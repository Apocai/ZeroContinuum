
Phalinas is a gifted boy, looking to be in his late teens and is a young prodigy of the [[Acolytes|Acolytes]]. 

He is quite handsome, with ruffled brown hair, youthful features, and a lean, muscular body. He is also quite tall, around 6'3. 

**Theana**
Like all the Acolytes, Phalinas loves and fears [[Daemonium Theana]], their master. 


**Acolytes**

Phalinas has a deep camaraderie with his fellow Acolytes, considering them his brothers. While they would occasionally fight, they would apologize quickly and make up. Unlike Apocanyser, they found no jealousy or envy at the growth of others, nor at how that was rewarded by Theana; instead, they constantly pushed onwards to eventually reach such a level.
