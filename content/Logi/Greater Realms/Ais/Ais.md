The Ais are a general term for the descendants of the [[Gods]] of the [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms]]. They vary in appearance, depending on their [[Gods|Divine Father]] They, as the progeny of the Gods, are not of specific races, instead being characterized by their Aiasin and ancestry (i.e. individuals of one Aiasin will be called Spawn of (God's name)), or Child of (God), or (God)kin, etc. 

Their civilizations (called [[Aiasin]]) are less advanced than those of the [[Lesser Realms]] due to their strength and lack of real threats. However, they war constantly, as their Masters themselves vie for dominance. Wars occur mainly among two individual Aiasin, as alliances mean little to Gods.

Ais by nature keep to themselves. Breeding also tends to be done within an Aiasin, resulting in incest being less of a taboo occurrence (though it varies by Aiasin).

The Ais are interested in the Lesser Realms, due to reasons ranging from conquest to conversion. It is based on the whims of their Divine Master deities, and also what the Ais feel would benefit it. Some have rooted themselves secretly into Lesser Realm nations or created their own proxy kingdoms (these tend to be theocracies).

**Abilities**: 
Individually, they are quite strong. Born from an environment of incredibly dense Etherion, all Ais are by default [[Etharii]]. They are incredibly attuned to Etherion, and are able to their Divine Father's [[Chain]] to a highly proficient level, not to mention their own gifts resulting from their divine blood (varies based on the God). They have no lifespan, and tend to have their physical aging dwindle as teens, upon which it slows down until it completely stops when they look to be in their middle ages. 

They suffer a loss in Etherion capacity when going to the Lower Realms, due to its lesser Etherion density. As Ais are beings of Etherion, this leads to a weakened state. The effects of this weakening can be reduced through training, done by limiting the Etherion that enters one's body while in the Greater Realms.

**Family**:
While this varies greatly by Aiasin, many of the Ais have an unique concept of family. As all are descended from their respective God, Ais tend to be more close-knit with members of their Aiasin. 

There is some level of respect and authority given to one's blood parent, but ultimately it is more sibling-like, as they all consider their deity to be their true parent. As a result, marriage itself is more loosely based, with polygamy a common practice. Marriage is more about choosing partner(s) to mutually aid one another rather than romantic love.

As mentioned previously, incest is not very taboo. They are immune to all diseases and genetic disorders, and as such suffer no consequence from repeated inmixing. The status of blood means little beneath the shadow of their God.
