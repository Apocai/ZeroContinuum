RED stands for Regenerative Etherionic Drink. It is the equivalent of a healing potion able to heal damages to [[Panhumanity|panhuman]] bodies ([[Manah]] are too different biologically). It is separated into different levels: Low, Medium, High, and Ultra, which dictate the efficacy of the healing.  

Low and Medium REDs were created during [[Timeline#^2ccc71|Etherius]] in (TBD), but didn't see much use till [[Timeline#^e9b1ac|Dominus]]. 
A key point to note is that the regeneration caused by RED cause extreme pain, which increases with the level of the RED. It is not uncommon for people to even die from taking higher level REDs (most often being High and Ultra). 

Low RED is used to heal injuries up to broken bones in a matter of hours. Medium RED can heal up to smaller amputations (i.e. fingers and toes) in the same amount of time. High RED can heal missing limbs in a matter of seconds. Ultra RED can heal almost from the point of death in seconds as well. 

As once can see, High and Ultra REDs are so painful because of the speed with which they heal. 

#### Usage:

Low and Medium REDs are available with a license (which can be obtained by anyone with training). The reason for such measures is that the body must be correctly set before being administered the medicine, so as to avoid incorrect healing (just like how bone fractures must be set correctly to heal).

High REDs can only be used by medicinal practitioners, and only with consent from the injured or (in the case where they are incapacitated) close family. High REDs are also only a last resort, due to risk of further injury, bodily trauma, or even death. High REDs see most of their use on the battlefield, where one must sustain such a risk to survive. 

Ultra REDs are banned almost all across the [[Lesser Realms]]. They boast a high fatality rate, and are for lack of a better word overpowered. There is rarely any need to go so far as to use them, as other measures are safer, if not quite as effective. It was created more as an experimental drug than anything. 

[[Lynx|Lynx's]] with healing [[Cipher|Ciphers]] are far more preferred over REDs, but they are quite rare. 

#### How it Works:

The basic principle involves ingesting a liquid that temporarily increases Etherion affinity and allows for the immediate creation of artificial stem cells that seek out and repair the body. Overtime, these stem cells are replaced by the body's own individual cells. 

REDs were created as an accidental byproduct when making Etherion enhancement drugs (which ultimately did not work).
