## **Appearance**

Apocanyser is a young man who looks to be in his late teens to early twenties. He has bold features, with heavy eyebrows and a strong nose, coupled with a defined jawline and prominent cheekbones. He is clean shaven, with olive skin and messy jet-black hair. He has tired, steel grey eyes, with light eyebags underneath. 

His body is quite muscular, akin to a martial artist's. However, he possesses the Heretical Physique, which includes new muscle groups, concentrated in the back, core, arms, and legs. Such a physique is  seen as unnatural and terrifying, but such is the price of strength.

##### Clothing:

His clothing is selected and approved by the [[Warden]], and as such is one worn by nobility, if somewhat altered.
