LAW is a series of laws created by [[ORDER]] which is the foundation of [[Creation]]

Some notable ones incude:
1) <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">The Strong Shall Oppress the Weak</span>
		This edict refers to how the stronger force "oppresses" the weaker force, from things like the physics, to nature, and even society. 
2) <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">Thou Shalt Not Defy the Laws of Physics (4 Fundamental Forces of the Universe)</span>
		These are the natural, scientific laws that humans know in real life. Breaking these is not like "defying gravity" through flight. It is a complete and utter violation of the laws, which cannot even be theorized about it because logic says it is impossible. 
3) <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">The Gods shall not escape their prison in body and soul</span>
4) <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0)">The soul and body make up one's being; both exist in tandem, and neither shall break without the other.</span>

The punishment for breaking LAW is to become the enemy of ORDER - becoming a [[Gods|God]]

LAW by its nature cannot be broken, as even Creation itself will correct itself to follow LAW. Therefore, to break LAW is to ascend beyond reason and logic; to defy the balance of ORDER.

LAW itself is only known in fragments. Many of its edicts (far more than listed here) are either undiscovered, or comprehendible.

As much of the [[Logi]] is unaware of LAW, and indeed, even ORDER, they have no way of knowing it except to find out themselves. 

Still, some fragments come from [[Zenelith]], though it is told through word of mouth rather than document. The question remains as to where such information originated.
