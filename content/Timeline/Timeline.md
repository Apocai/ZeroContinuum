
This is the timeline of [[Zenelith]]. Zenelith is the only [[World Factor|World]] that survives throughout the [[Continuum Infinus]], and as such, has an almost infinite history. Note that *only* the world (as in the World Factor) survives, so Zenelith's physical structure is only as old as the Infinus's current iteration. 

As such, this timeline is regarding the present iteration: the Zero Continuum.

> [!timeline|t-r t-5] **Infernus -  Age of Heavenly Fire** _10 million years ago _
> This is the Age that was begun and ended by the [[Great Sundering]], the war fought between the [[Gods]] in both the [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms]] and [[Lesser Realms]]. As the name implies, it was a time of through which Zenelith itself was cleansed with a sea of divine flame, wiping clean all that lived on the surface. Indeed, even the [[World Factor#^081645|Wul-Ral]] of Zenelith wasn't enough to prevent the flame from erasing all that was.
> 
> The war's end was signaled through the massive explosion named the [[Kuzarian Hammerblow]] that sent great swaths of ash and dust into the atmosphere, ushering in an age of darkness. 


> [!timeline|t-l t-3] **Cinis - Age of Ash** _6 milion years ago_
> Continuing immediately after the Hammerfall was the Age of Ash, a period of time where the resulting ash and dust blotted the sky fully. Much of the life that tried hiding under the surface perished from a lack of nourishment. 
>
> The Ais of [[The Bleeding King]], the God whose defeat was echoed by the Hammerblow, began to inhabit the [[Lesser Realms]], around modern day  driven from the [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms]]. These Ais, having lost much of their divinity and powers, became known as humans. 
> They dug deep within the ground, hoping to find some way to escape the wasteland that was the surface; alas, they found nothing, and it was only thanks to their decaying superhuman physicality that they managed to survive through this age, though their numbers decreased significantly.

> [!timeline|t-r t-3] **Varsus - Age of  Varsha** _ 3 million years ago_
> The effects of the Great Sundering were healing, and Zenelith could be showered in the light of [[Ral-Varsha]] once more. But of course, humanity, who over the course of 3 million years had lost every spec of divinity they once possessed, were left behind in the development of the world.
> 
> As life blossomed once more on Zenelith, humanity would quickly find out that they were outmatched in both power, vitality, and tenacity. With life came the return of the  [[Akashraal]], powerful, intelligent creatures that found humanity to be easy prey. Humanity's days were spent being hunted by these beasts, their days  marked with fear and terror. 
> 
> It was also during this time that other [[Logi]],  including [[Manah]] and [[Panhumanity|Panhumans]], began to rise. 

> [!timeline|t-l t-2] **Logus - Age of  Logoi** _ 1 million years ago_
> The most influential aspect of this age was the growth and progression of non-human [[Logi]]. While the history between all Logoi were marked with conflict and distrust (some more than others) in the early days, they had little choice but to work together to survive the harsh lands.  
>
> The power of the unified Logoi helped in creating the first early nomadic civilizations.

> [!timeline|t-l t-2] **Strivus - Age of  Strife** _  500 thousand years ago_
> With young civilizations growing across Zenelith, and Logoi adapting to the Akashraal, their focus quickly turned to one another. Or rather, they would have, had not a more pressing concern taken hold.
> 
> As Zenelith slowly began returning to the Etherion-rich environment it once was, panhumans began developing [[Etharii]] within their ranks: people born with affinity for Etherion. While such powers promised a new age of innovation and cultivation, the resulting madness denied all such prospects. Without the knowledge or means to successfully train and [[Chain]] with [[Chain#^9b2de5|Masters]], Etharii had no other option but to descend into insanity, unable to withstand the corruption of the [[Gods]].   
> 
> While Etharii began forming within other species, it is with humans where they blossomed. Their direct ancestry to the Ais of the Bleeding King made them especially receptacle to Etherion, making the Etharii madness far more prevalent. As a result, humans were shunned by the rest of the panhumans.
>
> However, the ones who suffered the most were the Manah, the beings completely unrelated to humanity. Unlike panhumanity, they possess no relation whatsoever to the Bleeding King's Ais, and thus had no defenses against Etherion. To them Etherion was poisonous and destructive to their body, leading many to perish. It wasn't long before the Manah went extinct, vanishing from Zenelith. 
> 
> In wake of the new power gap created by the Manah, alongside the fear and panic that had taken root with the Etharii madness, large wars between panhumans began occuring, even despite the ongoing risk of Akashraal. Humans, who were the most isolated, were hit the hardest.

> [!timeline|t-r t-3] **Brightus - Age of  Bright** _ 3000 years ago_
> After the Kindling came a period of relative stagnation, caused by many factors, among them being the fear of Etherion. As the rate of Etharii births increased, so did the widespread panic regarding Etherion. Very few [[Lynx|Lynxes]] developed, and even those hardly progressed beyond Tier-I. With no way of knowing surefire ways to Chain with the higher beings and a growing population of madmen, it led to an age of persecution, culling, and distrust. 
> 
> Eventually, panhumanity uncovered how to convert[[Ortherium|Ortherium]], a rare mineral formed in Etherion-dense environments into a highly efficient energy source known as [[Bright]]. Ortherium was actually discovered during Strivus, but was seen simply as a rare, magic rock that glowed prettily. Now, realizing that by melting and purifying the ore with concentrated light from the Ral-Varsha, Bright was created. At first, liquid Bright was used as a light source adjacent to oil (due to the Sundering, fossil fuels are almost completely non-existent). As innovation grew, the first Bright engine was created, which used combustion to heat water into steam. However, an Industrial Revolution wasn't possible due to civilizations inability to settle permanently in a location, due of course to the Akashraal. Still, they persevered, little by little advancing farther into the realm of science.
>
> It comes to no surprise that panhumanity after a few centuries were able to discover Bright's potential for direct energy release (i.e. be used not as a medium but as a source of energy). Electricity was discovered a while before, yet it was unable to gain significant backing due to Bright's greater accessibility and portability. With this new technology, man began establishing the first permanent civilizations.

> [!timeline|t-r t-1] **Etherius - Age of  Etherion** _ 1000 years ago_
> Though Bright technology progressed panhuman civilization, the issue of Etharii had yet to be resolved. Organizations like the [[Exedis Sanctum]] were created to aid them, though they were dismissed as lunatics and heretics. This changed with the birth a young girl named [[Zul-Irinth-Kazyn]], in [[Thoran-Zul|Thoran]].
> 
> It was an old, weak city, filled with poverty and anarchy. Yet, in but a few centuries, with the leadership of the [[Tempering#^c1348a|Visionary]] Irinth, it became a behemoth regarded today as one of the strongest nations not just in Zenelith, but the entirety of the Lesser Realms.
> 
> Irinth paved the way for Etherion enlightenment, which helped develop and create many of prototypes and early models of equipment used today. However, her greatest accomplishment would easily be the integration and regulation of Exeids Sanctums, and consequently, the production of Lynxes. And with Lynxes came a whole new level of firepower for Logoi. And this allowed Logoi to do what they never could before: begin their counterattack against the Akashraal. 
> 
> Of course, they would never dare try to wipe out all Akashraal, as such a feat is beyond even modern civilization today. But still, they were able to overcome the oppression of Akashraal, paving their way to true advancement.

> [!timeline|t-l t-1] **Dominus - Age of  Dominion** _ 160 years ago (Present Day)_
> This was perhaps the greatest Age in all of Zenelithian history. And it was marked by a single creation that changed Creation forever: the [[Fluxgate|Fluxgate]]. This device made possible interstellar travel, allowing Zenelith to begin the colonizing of other worlds. Such a feat brought it with it heaps of advancements and understandings, and revolutionized Zenelith. It is here where Etherion technology became mainstream, though its risk carried with it a fair amount of distrust and caution. As such, other, older technology still remained prominent - chiefly Bright. Still, with every new discovery and innovation, Etherion began to be more and more implemented into society, paving the way for the prosperity of panhumans.
