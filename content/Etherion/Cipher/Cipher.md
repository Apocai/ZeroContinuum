An Etherion cipher (also known as a spell), is the usage of Etherion through a predefined blueprint (via [[Hypnochanting]]) to achieve an objective. It requires great concentration, with the slightest deviation leading to the failure of the spell (effects vary based on program intent. This can range from burns, electrocution, or nausea all the way [[Gate]] fracturement. 

In order to cast a cipher, a Lynx must cycle Etherion through their Gates, starting from their [[Etharii#^301ca9|Third Eye]], while using hypnochanting to pour the cycled Etherion through the envisioned blueprint, which casts the cipher. Note that while the blueprint is imaginary, it is through that which the cipher operates. Thus, the blueprint must stand clear as day within the user's head, without discrepancies or faults, lest the cipher break. 

In order to maintain concentration in tense situations, rigorous training is done in [[Exedis Sanctum|Exedis Sanctums]], among which hypnochanting is taught. 

***
The Ciphers one can learn and use are from a [[Lynx|Lynx's]] [[Chain]]. While each Chain has unique Ciphers, there are some common Ciphers shared through different Chains, each specialized slightly to their Chain. For instance, Transmutation transforms one object to another. One Chain can have a Transmutation Cipher that changes all that is effected to steel, while another could change everything to bone.
