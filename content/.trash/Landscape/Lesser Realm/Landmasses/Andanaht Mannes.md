The Andanaht Mannes is an unexplored region that lies beyond the [[Mountains of Duske]], boasting an incredibly high Etherion density, it likewise has many strange Etherion phenomena, and is inhabited by great [[Etherbeast|Etherbeasts]], though there were rumors of other, even worse things lurking in the shadows. Whatever the case, the region has long since been forbidden to the man, due to its danger, and the risk of becoming infected by *something* that could be spell doom were it to be spread to others.
