
A Chain is the creation of a "chain" binding a [[Lynx]] to a Master. Through this Chain, a Lynx's becomes attuned to their Master, allowing them access to the [[Cipher|Ciphers]] within. 

A Chain is more than a bridge to a few Ciphers however. It is said that a Chain is a bridge to divinity; a path to ascension. For Chain is not just a link between Lynx and Master, but a level of *connection*. As one increases their mastery over the Chain, so to they draw closer to their Master. And as they draw closer, they become something...greater, something more. Alas, few men have ever reaches such realms, and so such tales lie as but legends. Nevertheless, it is the goal of all who call themselves Lynxes to reach such a realm, to achieve such a state.

#####  Masters:

^2013cb

^9b2de5
A Lynx must forge a Chain with a Master, typically a higher being including Gods, Divine Akashraal, etc. In exchange for a small seed of their power, a Lynx must devote their life in service (whether physically or spiritually), in service of their Master. This is typically done by following edicts or codes set by the Master, specific actions, etc. and is known as the Master's **Ideal**.

In return, Masters feed of off these bonds, gaining strength and influence. This is incredibly important for Gods in particular, as they maintain their sanity and sense of self through their influence.

While who or what can become a Master isn't known, it is generally accepted that a Master must have incredible powerful and a deep connection to Etherion.

##### Shackling:

^a42976
A Shackling or Shacklement is the process through which a Lynx forges a Chain to a Master. The specifics of the ritual varies from Master to Master, though it generally involves the completion of a specific act. 

The location where a Shackling takes place is crucial. For most higher beings, it takes place in altars or vast temples dedicated to them; others (as in the case of some Divine Akashraal) must be done in their presence. 

Of course, that is not the end. After said completion, the Master will peer deep into the soul of the Lynx, judging whether they are deserving of the Master's grace. It is a forceful opening-up of the Lynx's innermost thoughts and desires, a complete violation of their very self. And indeed, especially in the case of the Gods, it is an invasion by a presence no mortal presence can hope to comprehend. While uncommon, it said some have died from the mere presence of the Master.

Of course, not all Masters are equal: the weaker and lesser-known ones have higher success rates in successful Shackling, while the more well-known Masters are considerably less so. It is not merely pride and arrogance that makes Shackling with them so hard, but rather the fact they are so ancient their very being feels alien - especially considering age often correlates to madness. 

But in that case, why would anyone want to Chain to such Masters? The reason is simple: power. It is said that the older, stronger Masters offer stronger Chains. And with stronger Chains come stronger abilities, stronger power. Of course, the risks are great as well. Even if one somehow passes their Shacklement to such a being, what they request of you may be well beyond your abilities or reason. And if you cannot fill such a desire then.... 

##### Ideal:

A Master's Ideal is the culmination of their very being: their desires, goals, and way of life. It may not seem obvious, or indeed, even known to the Master themselves; yet, it is the fundamental representation of what they are.

A Lynx, once Chained to a Master, must follow this Ideal. What drives the Master must now drive the Servant. Every Master has their own unique Ideal, though some may seem similar. While some Ideals are harmless, such as bathing in the light of the Ral-Varsha for ten minutes every day, others are notably less so - such as being mandated to kill regularly. 

It is for this reason that Chains are heavily regulated. Their are many mandates set by international and interworld agreements that ban the Shackling of certain Masters, due to the danger of their Ideals. And at the very top of this list stands the **Primechains**.

##### Selection

So then, how do Lynxes choose their Masters? Normally, it is one worshipped within one's family or community, and approved by the Lynx's [[Exedis Sanctum]]. It could also be one that the Lynx holds an affinity for, such as an incredibly distant ancestor. Lynxes who share an affinity with their Mastery tend to increase their Mastery with the Chain faster, and even unlock the hidden potential within them. 

##### Primechains:

^7969a6
Among all outlawed Chains, Primechains stand at the top. Not only are the Ideals of their Masters incredibly dangerous, they are also immensely difficult to handle. It is nigh impossible to successfully complete a Shackling, and boast a high mortality rate. 

More often than not, these Masters are utterly insane, and as such will infect the Lynx with their madness, causing them to run rampant possessing power they cannot handle. Even the Shackling themselves are abhorrent rituals that require vile and gruesome deeds.

Of course, Primechains by their nature are powerful, which is why so many pursue them. However, holding a Primechains is subject for immediate extermination, and will cause all state and governments to cooperate to execute the wielder and contain the Primechain. 

The knowledge on the Shackling for some of these Primechains has been erased from history, while others are held in the smallest, darkest places within a nation.
