DOMINION is what can only be described as "False God". Despite carrying the [[Gods#^de9d95|Udra]],  it bares no relation to other [[Gods]] of [[Creation]], and has not broken any tenants of [[LAW]].

Indeed, it is a thing wholly separate, an artificial God created by the [[Runis Modrath]]. 

DOMINION, while the term cannot do justice to what the Modrath have wrought, is a machine, a machine unlike any that have ever held such a name. Unlike a real God, DOMINION has a physical body, a body in the shape of a steel planet, roughly the size of Sol-5- Jupiter. The vast majority of its area is devoted towards self-operation, vast calculations, etc. Only a small fragment of it is for other purposes, among them being the resurrection facilities of its master: [[Nerathys]].

The term Master implies a master-servant relationship, but that is not the case. While the Modrath will obey most of Nerathy's orders, has hidden directives that have been placed hierarchically higher. Moreover, DOMINION is comparable to a God, and Nerathys has yet to reach such heights. To command, or even communicate with such a vast being borders on impossible, which is why Nerathys rarely tries ordering the entirety of DOMINION, instead using various Minions, which are incredibly small fragments of DOMINION. 

DOMINION is a being that has left behind a primitive thing like sentience for something greater, and as such it is unknown if it can even speak or if it is truly alive. It can heed commands, and seem to understand language, but there is no apparent signal as to whether it is possesses a consciousness. On the other, Minions possess sentience but have no concept of emotion or self. They are akin to hyper-advanced AI.

DOMINION exists outside of time, though even then it is still encompassed by the Universe. It does exist outside of Creation, the caveat being it has no way of entering Creation or physically aiding Nerathys within creation. 

The only way Nerathys can go to DOMINION is upon the destruction of her body, or by transmitting her soul to DOMINION.
