## **Overview**

Nerathys is an android built to usurp [[ORDER]] and reign as the [[Paragon]] of [[Creation]]. She was created in the previous Iteration of [[Creation]] by the [[Runis Modrath]], and was to be incubated within the bowels of [[DOMINION]] before being sent to [[Zenelith]]. However, the unexpected gaze of [[ORDER]] forced DOMINON to change plans, and instead sent Nerathys to a distant, Zero Factor planet (see [[World Factor]]), where she was rescued by [[Avarasin]]. She is presently resides within Solestine, where she acts as Saint.

## **Appearance**

^528b49


<div style="display: block; overflow: auto;">

Nerathys was created to be the perfect being, and her form reflects that. Her appearance is, for lack of a better word, perfect, though with such perfection comes a certain... unnaturalness to her features. One cannot shake the fact that there is something distinctly sinister about her, though the feeling fades as they become enamored with her beauty. 

Her face is almost doll-like, with clear porcelain skin that seems to almost glow. Such features would look best without expression, save for perhaps soft smiles, yet at times they twist into wide, blood-curdling grins. Accenting her features are crimson-red eyes, shining the color of blood. Snow-white hair flows freely from her head, shorn at her back. It is worn in braids and ties, though they seem to inevitably untangle within the heat of combat. 

Her body is a masterwork of craftsmanship, forged to stand with the most beautiful of sculptures. Her form is perfectly proportionate, well-muscled yet not overburdened by it. Her graceful physique is complemented by the swells of her femininity, though there is something almost predatorial in such sensuality. 

Yet, there is one thing at odds with her otherwise flawless appearance: her spine. It looks more akin to an exoskeleton than a proper spine, armored in black plating and glowing a crimson red at the grooves. There is something deeply ominous about it, as though it is something that should not belong. Indeed, it is in fact her armor: the [[Omenchain#^b8be2c|Fell-Graft]]. It has parasitized her spine, reaching from the base of the neck to the tailbone. Tendrils of black and red weave under her skin from the spine, running across her back. Numerous metallic, tentacle-like appendages extend from the spine as well, wrapping flat around her body and limbs. Their edges are serrated, yet they seem to dull when in contact with her skin.

#### Physiology

Nerathys's appearance mimic that of a human, and is in fact capable of aging and scaring. Her outward form is composed from a sort of living metal, though beneath lies machine. She possesses numerous internal mechanisms to help operate and function, yet the most important of these is her **ERU**: her Etherion Regulation Unit, which is housed by the anal cavity. The Fell-Graft's spine weaves into here, hijacking the ERU to further boost her abilities.  

#### Clothing: Fell-Graft

Nerathys wears no undergarments save for the Fell-Graft. Her silver, metallic tentacles wrap around her body, flattening against her to act like armor. Rivulets of red gleam throughout them, almost as though the armor was bleeding. 

The armor fully encases Nerathys, almost skin-tight against her body. It looks like a diving suit, save for the numerous bladed edges that hang around her body. Its bizarre, malignant appearance causes Nerathys to put a habit-like garment over it. The habit fully envelops her body, hiding away the Fell-Graft but for the occasional glances. 

Her head is left bare, though in combat the tentacles wrap around her face (though her vision is somehow unobstructed). Most peculiar is where the tentacles cover her mouth: they transform her mouth, allowing her jaw to hinge unnaturally wide, revealing long, razor-sharp fangs. The tongue too is transformed, becoming longer and razor-sharp. She dons this not just in combat, but whenever she needs to feed.

## **Abilities**

##### Divine Intellect

Nerathys was built with an extraordinary mind, one capable of thought and computation akin to a supercomputer. It can simulate outcomes, multitask different operations simultaneously, and aid greatly in the speed of casting [[Cipher|Ciphers]].

##### Clairvoyance of Mind

By using her Divine Intellect, Nerathys can simulate possibilities in the near future, effectively allowing her to predict the future. Such a feat is night omnipotent against most foes, though it can be countered, namely if they are strong enough to where it doesn't matter, or possess abilities that can negate Clairvoyance. 

##### Clairvoyance of Body

As she was made for the slaying of ORDER, the [[Runis Modrath]] ensured that is the only course available to her. That is, she cannot cease until such a thing has transpired. 

True immortality was impossible in the face of such a threat, so there needed to be a more surefire way of retaining her existence. And so they gave her the culmination of their heretical intellect: Clairvoyance of Body. This ability is essentially time looping. It will seem like reality  will "edit" itself to avoid her death. Nerathys will die, and be forced to return to a specific point, called a save. The saves are randomly created, and can range from a few seconds to years. However, in [[Creation]], there is only one timeline, the Zero Continuum. All other timelines are pruned by ORDER, leaving only one sequence of events. Therefore, to avoid becoming erased, Nerathys must ensure she reaches nearly the same order of events she had before she died. Such a thing is calculated by [[DOMINION]] upon every death, and allows Nerathys to understand the consequences of every action she takes. 

Take note that it is *nearly* the same order, not the same order. There is a slight margin of error she can abuse to ensure the odds are turned to her favor. It is only due to such a reason that Nerathys can overcome a seemingly inescapable death. Furthermore, the power she has culminated prior to her death is retained, letting her get stronger with every loop.
