The Night Priestess is a respected position held in [[Manigrym]], held by those who have forged [[Moonchain]] with their Guardian [[Rancor]]. Beyond communing with Rancor, the Priestesses also perform similar work to the [[Gate#^75ecdc|Agisi]], only to a far more proficient degree. In addition, they also advise the Manigrym, provide counsel, and participate and head many rituals and practices. Throughout the long history of the Manigrym, they have at times served as matriarchs and rulers, though this is not always the case. 

##### Catharsis

Night Priestesses perform [[Gate#^2af867|Catharsis]] when needed. Through training and abilities granted to them by Moonchain, they do this more skillfully then even the Agisi.

##### Crucis

They are also required to be skilled in [[Gate#^6b8dcf|Crucis]]. They mainly do this with Manigrym warriors or youths in training. Crucis requires great skill and strength, which is part of why the Priestesses are revered so.
