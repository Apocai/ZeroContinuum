A Lynx refers to a [[Etharii]] that have forged a [[Chain]] and have graduated from a [[Exedis Sanctum]].

Lynxes specialize in different [[Chain|Chains]], through which they become something *beyond* man. While more can be read in the Chains page, through a Chain, a Lynxes body, including their brain, changes to better utilize their Chains. The effects can vary depending on the Chain and how far a Lynx has progressed with it, but changes include eye color, hair color, appendages, physical strength, speed, stamina, etc. As such, they are both respected and feared by various cultures throughout the Lesser Realms. 

They have also been trained in Sanctums to control their [[Gate|Gates]], cycling and shaping them to increase their efficacy. The sign of a skilled Lynx is how well they can control their Gates. 

**Biology**

The more powerful and attuned one is to their [[Chain]], the more they are warped by the influence of their Divine Master. The changes vary depending on the individual Divine Master, but all are carry an air of otherworldliness. 

***
The [[Ais]] of the Greater Realms, while technically Etharii as they have not been trained officially in the Lesser Realms, stand head and shoulders above the Lesser Realm denizens in terms of power and aptitude. 

This is mainly attributed to their incredibly Etherion-dense environment, divine blood, and extremely high number of gates. 

An Ais exhibits no physical changes through their Chain, as their body is already fully adapted to it (as they must use the Chain of their respective God).
