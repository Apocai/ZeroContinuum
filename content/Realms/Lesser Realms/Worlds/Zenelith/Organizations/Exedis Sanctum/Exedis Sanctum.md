
An Exedis Sanctum is a school for [[Etharii]], divided into the First and Second Colleges. ^e2e392

Exedis Sanctums have been around for much of history, though they have been shunned for their relative distaste of non-Etharii and practice of many distasteful and morally dubious arts. 

As a result, they were shunned by society, and were often found in secluded regions far from civilization. Their students were mostly outcasts: the persecuted, orphans, criminals, and runways. As Etharii have no choice but to learn to control their power and become [[Lynx|Lynxes]], they had little choice but to swear body and soul to their Sanctum.
 
 With [[Timeline#^2ccc71|Etherius]], this has changed, as now nations readily hunt for new [[Lynx|Lynxes]]. Exedis Sanctums have been reintroduced to society, albeit with severe governmental regulations. 

Exedis Sanctums are now run by respective national governments, though almost all readily welcome international and interstellar Etharii. 

Upon graduating the Second College, a Lynx is mandated to comply with their Sanctum's order and register with the affiliated nation's government. They will then begin working for the nation, in various governmental programs. These can range from research, military, or special task forces, depending on their specializations. Failure to comply leads to an immediate arrest, upon which they are forced to comply or are executed. 

When working with a nation, Lynxes are given a high stipend, and are guaranteed many benefits for themselves and their families.  

##### First College

The First College is seen as the preparatory state for potential [[Lynx|Lynxes]], a student. It is started as soon as the Etharii potential is seen within a child, usually around 12 years. Through the next 6 years, they will begin study, learning various topics such as mathematics, history, science, language, etc. Furthermore, they also trained in a variety of martial techniques and physically conditioning to help control their [[Gate|Gates]], a key factor in their upcoming Chaining. All the while, they will do various meditations and take drugs to keep their sanity until the Chaining. 

They are also taught [[Gate#^2af867|Catharsis]], as well as receive Catharsis. They do not need to be as skilled as an [[Gate#^75ecdc|Agisi]], so long as they know enough to function. 

There are few fatalities within the First College, as the process and education has been improved upon time and time again throughout its long history. Still, it does happen rarely, mostly for those who have not been admitted on time or have unusually high Etherion affinity. Such cases require them to be put in a special program, one far more intensive than normal. 

Upon finishing their 6 years, initiates will complete their graduation by Chaining to a Divine Master Master Master of their choice (or one recommended by the Sanctum). There is a rate of failure of around 5%, usually from angering the Divine Master, not meeting the Divine Master's qualifications, or messing up the Chaining. 

Those who have successfully completed the Chaining and have become Lynxes are then directed to enter into the Second College. 

##### Second College

The Second College is where graduates learn to use their Chain and begin the slow road to mastering it. The Second College is divided into separate chambers, each specializing in a Chain or group of Chains. Apprentices join one, and then spend the next 4 years gaining adequate mastery over their Chain (usually to Tier II), before graduating as full fledged Lynxes.
