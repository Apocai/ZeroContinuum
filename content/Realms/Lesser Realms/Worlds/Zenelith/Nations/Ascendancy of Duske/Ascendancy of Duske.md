Duske is a nation within Zenelith that borders the [[Andanaht Mannes]], separated by a mountain range named the [[Taryld Duske]]. 

## Overview 

Duske is ruled by [[Lunthiel|Lunthiel the Ascendent]], and possesses a deep technological and Etherion foundation. It is a relatively recent nation, but is highly feared due to its military strength, a large part of which is due to the Duske [[Executioners]].
