## **Background**

Titan. They are the rulers of Duske, named after the ancient Titans who's blood was said to run in the veins of the Duskans. And, like their namesake, so to must they be fierce and mighty. Duske is a hard land, and so they must be harder. 

Arin lived to embody such a concept. Born the eldest child of the Vael Audaunis, she was groomed from birth for such a role. Every waking moment was spent studying politics, diplomacy, international relations, governance, and even seduction and the bedroom arts. She was also trained by many [[Combatsmith|combatsmiths]], and quickly became proficient in the arts of war. 

Duske did not have very favorable relations, due in part to their large population of [[Onikris]] and religion. Moreso, they were situated in [[Varithyl]], alone but for the region of [[Andanaht Mannes]] that bordered it. Surrounded by sea and mountain, away from the main continent. Duske struggled to survive and carve out its seat in the political world. And Arin must one day govern such a role. Even as a child, she was forced to live under such pressure, with no other option but to overcome it. 

When Arin was nine years old, she was sent along with her half-sister Lunthiel to a secluded facility, followed with a retinue of trusted servants and instructors. There, they trained together, up until Arin had to leave for the [[Royal Academy of Laitskaer]] at age fourteen. She returned five years later to assume the throne following her father Daymion's death

 While at first the Audaunis tried to control her as a puppet (believing her to hold the same naivety she did in her youth)I, they were sorely mistaken. Arin proved immensely capable, wresting away control from her family and quickly assuming the role of Titan. She was supported only by her mother Zynaria, who continued to support Arin with her advice and political savviness. Still, amidst a sea of enemies, most from Vaels (including her own), Arin began to change. Where once was a hardworking, yet passionate girl stood only a cold-heartened woman. She hardened herself, taking on the mantle of Titan and casting away Arin the girl. Such a change was not lost on others, who begun to fear her callous and hardened nature. Her merciless response to enemies and rebellion, the ease with which she ordered executions, and her meticulous ethic gained her the moniker Iron Wolf. Yet with that came a sudden realization - the more Arin worked for Duske, and less she began to love it. It was a slow, gradual change, but as Arin began to uncover the darker secrets of Duske, her early patriotism began to drain away. Entrenched as she was into the lies, deceit, and conspiracies, she began to think of her rule as no more than a duty she must fulfill, without emotion or love. It was only thanks to her mother that Arin did not begin to take harsher measures against those who would stand in her way, and retain her humanity.   

Later, she took on a squire named Sinari who who eventually became her Lorde Consort, following the reveal of his royalty. She did so to secure a relationship with the [[Manigrym]], which was bolstered by their Guardian [[Rancor]] deciding to let Arin forge Moonchain with it. Due to that, Arin was to be the next [[Night Priestess]] of the Manigrym, and was trained by the former Night Priestess in their ways.

## **Personality**

Arin was once a noble, kindhearted girl who's diligence and kindness earned the admiration of those around her. She had a large following backed not just her status but her charisma, which grew through her time at the [[Royal Academy of Laitskaer]]. But, as she became Titan, her naivety disappeared, and she became the person the world knew as the Wolf of Duske. 

Assuming her role, she grew cold and severe. Her judgement sharp, her words fierce, it was said her walk through the hallways of the [[Duske Spire]] were more akin to the hunter's prowl of a wolf rather than a woman. Besieged on all sides by enemies, she learned to cast away Arin to assume the Titan, discarding whatever was needed to better fulfill her role. Her demeanor, once open and warmhearted, grew to be haughty and intimidating. Her charisma remained, though people were now attracted by her strength and ferocity rather than her kindness. Her temper has also gotten notably shorter, though she masks it well with her frigid expression. Small, tell-tale signs could be seen, from the steeliness of her eyes, the slight downward curl of her lips, and her silence. 

Most prominent was perhaps her increasing hate for Duske. She began to despise the squabbling Lordes and Vaels, the greedy merchants and companies, and the scheming inside and out of Duske. Coupled with some of their "savage" customs, reputation as barbarians, and arrogance, she began loathing her nation. Entrenched as she was in the lies, deceit, and conspiracies, she began to think of her rule as no more than a duty she must fulfill, without emotion or love. It was only thanks to her mother and Lunthiel that Arin did not begin to take harsher measures against those who would stand in her way, and retain her humanity.  


## **Abilities**


##### Combat Ability:

Arin is a formidable warrior, owing to her natural talent and hard work. She is skilled in hand-to-hand combat, and favors the the bow and spear. 

##### Moonchain

Arin, despite doing well in the academy and being an [[Etharii]], does not have much aptitude as a [[Lynx]]. She has forged [[Wardchain]], but has used her abilities to train her [[Gate|Gates]] rather than progress as a Lynx. 

Through union with [[Rancor]], Arin forged [[Moonchain]]. Normally, Lynxes cannot manage two Chains: owing to the unique nature of Chains relating to the [[Many-Fanged One]], Moonchain devoured Wardchain. 

##### Gates

Arin possesses 4 Gates, all of which are properly trained. 

She has maintained her maintenance and training since her days at the academy. However, after becoming the Night Priestess of the the Manigrym she also started regularly training through [[Gate#^6b8dcf|Crucis]], which helped greatly in improving her Gates. 

##### Titan

As the Titan, Arin is skilled in diplomacy, negotiation, clerical duties, among others.
