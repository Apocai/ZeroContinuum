## **Appearance**

Theana is a breathtaking woman, known throughout the Greater Realms as a lady of divine beauty. Her long, obsidian back hair is tied a half bun held by a exquisitely crafted hair pin, the rest of the hair cascading down to her legs. Her face itself is of an ageless quality and reminiscent of the moon, with its perfectly sculpted features. The eyes themselves are two clear pools of purple amethyst, a thing of beauty that freezes all that meet her gaze. But all that beauty is held behind a mask of cold indifference.

![[Theana 1.png| right lp| 500]]

 Theana's body, as befitting a [[Demigod]], is a thing of otherworldly craftsmanship. Her body is lithe yet powerful, with long, graceful limbs amidst smooth, perfectly worked muscles. 

Her backside is masterfully crafted, from the sharp shoulder blades to the gentle arch of her spine. Her back trails down into a gentle swell, revealing the lush curves of her hips.

Her beauty is only further articulated by her obsidian jewelry, each priceless treasures. A necklace hangs from her neck, reclining into her ample cleavage, while master worked armlets and anklets adorn her limbs. 

##### Clothing

Theana's clothing is one that accentuates her body, showing her pride and arrogance in being the First Demon King.

Her outfit resembles an altered qipao of  onyx [[Greatloom|greatloom]],  its dark color standing in sheer contrast to the jade skin its worn over. Traces of gold patterning weave throughout the dress, emphasizing its regal make. The occasional shimmer of Etherion, couples with the shifting patterns all serve to hint at its otherworldly creation. 

Shoulder-less, the qipao cups her bust low, its draped off-shoulder sleeves resting on her upper arms. The dress is open from the side, the gap running down until the hips where it stopped by a beautifully-worked waistbelt made of dragonhide. 

The dress is slitted at the sides below the belt, dividing the dress into a narrow front and backside. From the front hangs a long piece of fabric, ending loosely around her ankles. The backside's panel is mirrors the front design, and likewise dangles at the ankles.

Needless to say, a dress of such delicacy is entirely unsuited for combat. But those are the worries of lesser men. Someone of her caliber need not care for such things.
