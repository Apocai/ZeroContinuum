Etharii are those born with a unique [[Gate]] called the **Third Eye** in their forehead which allows for the visualization and manipulation of [[Etherion]]. They can only be born within worlds of a sufficient [[World Factor]], and have mostly been persecuted by society.  ^301ca9

Without proper instruction and guidance, through schools known as [[Exedis Sanctum|Exedis Sanctums]], Etharii slowly become insane, as they are unable to withstand the corruption brought by the influence of the [[Gods]], the price to pay for being attuned to Etherion. This is why all Etharii are required to form Chains with Gods and become [[Lynx|Lynxes]].

An Etharii that has lost all reason is a danger to both themselves and those around them, which is what led to their persecution before the later stages of[[Timeline#^2ccc71| Etherius]]. 

In the [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms]],  all [[Ais]] possess the Third Eye, and have no distinction like Etharii. Ais are usually instructed by family and have no need for things like [[Exedis Sanctum|Exedis Sanctums]]. 

#### Biology

Compared to other beings, Etharii possess a unique, slightly different biology. While they still require proper sustenance and hydration, as well as sleep, they no longer expel any bodily waste. Instead, their waste is replaced by pure, decayed Etherion (similar to what leads to the creation of [[Ortherium]]). This Etherion is urinated or sweated out (there are minor differences in the liquid, with the urine being higher concentrated Etherion). 

In fact, this excretion is highly sought by those manufacturing artificial Ortherium, and pay quite a hefty sum to collect regular samples.
