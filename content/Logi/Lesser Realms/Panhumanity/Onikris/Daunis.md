Daunis are a race of [[Panhumanity|Panhumans]] resulting from the offspring of [[Kynodus]] and [[Nesoren]]. They vary in appearance and traits, possessing skin tones ranging from the bronze skin of the Nesoren to lighter variants. They likewise possess various hair colors. 

They have relatively long lifespans, living around 220 to 250 years. 

#### Appearance

They can either pointed ears (like their Nesoren ancestors), or larger, furred Kynodian ears that start from the sides of their heads and extent to the top. Swaths of golden fur are found down the spine, collarbone, shoulders. 

They have facial features reminiscent of their ancestors, including golden eyes and sharp teeth.
