The Haven is a secret facility underneath the [[Duske Spire]], and is the headquarters of the [[Executioners]]. 

It does not only house the Executioners, but also various personnel from scientists, groundskeepers, [[Lynx|Lynxes]], caretakers, servants, etc. all of whom make up the Haven.
The Haven is under the direct control of the [[Lunthiel|Ascendant]], though it is commanded by [[Audaunis Arin|Titan Arin]]. 

Its facilities are great, with many training grounds for the Executioners, various labs were research is conducted, storage facilities for weaponry, and various other utilities. 

However, its greatest (and most expensive) facility is the **Nexus**, a series of [[Fluxgate|Fluxgates]] directed towards various interstellar colonies and potential colonies, as well as other worlds. 

It is maintained by the central control system [[Arsenal]].
