#### Overview:

The [[Saestan Islands]] are a series of islands south of [[Varithyl]] founded by the [[Nesoren]] and [[Kynodus]] clans in the [[Lesser Realms]]. It was once a city-state that that struck out onto the mainland to claim the region of Duske, before deciding to settle there and make the Seastan Islands into a terrirtory

Presently, it serves as a vassal state to the [[Ascendancy of Duske]], and is ruled by the Rhaskan [[Vael]]. 

#### History:

When the [[Nesoren]] first arrived to the Forgotten Isles from across the [[Dead Sea]], they beheld a tribe of beastkin, the [[Kynodus]] tribe already inhabiting the Isles, though they consisted off only around a hundred members, while the Nesoren had triple that. While [[Onikris]] reputation wasn't very favorable during that era as well, the Nesoren were also a group of outcasts, and did not share the same bias. 

In addition, the Nesoren quickly noticed the state of decline the Kynodus tribe lived in, witnessing the malnourished physique upon their bodies. When asked, it was said to be the result of their lack of compatibility with the Isle's natural ecosystem, as they were unsuited to fish or swim in the harsh Dead Sea. They had subsisted entirely off of the wild akashraal that had inhabited the Isles, but even that wasn't enough. Kynodus were slowly dying out from having too small a population, and theorized that they would only last a few more generations. They could not leave, as they were widely hunted in the mainland, and had no choice but exist here. 

The Nesoren eventually devised a plan, that would help both groups. The Nesoren were well suited to fishing, farming, and had trained Etharii. On the other hand, the Kynodus were a powerful warrior-tribe, that could easily fend off akashraal and other attackers. 

Thus, they would coexist, living together as one people. To solve the repopulation issue, the Nesoren proposed the following based on the Kynodus's genealogy: 

-Kynodus, when mating with other races, will genetically have the Kynodian female give birth to a mixed-race while the Kynodian male's sperm would result in a pure-blooded Kynodus.

Therefore:

1) Nesoren men will take two wives, one Nesoren and one Kynodus. This will result in Nesoren and mixed ([[Daunis]]) children. 
2) Kynodus men will also take two wives, both Nesoren. This will result in Kynodus offspring.
3) Mixed male offspring will be able to take up to two of their preference. The offspring will always be Daunis.

This plan was made due to the male-oriented nature of the two societies. However, it would solve both issues.

## Society:

Their society can be split into two eras: Pre and Post Unification.

##### <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0)">Pre-Unification:</span>

**Overview**
This time period was characterized by Audaunis's relative isolation from the rest of the world. Left unhindered, they developed practices that have remained untampered with and unrestrained for centuries.

First and foremost, Pre-Unification (and to some extent Post Unification) is a highly androcentric society. There have been women in positions of authority, and are not viewed as lesser; only that societal and cultural norms are often biased towards males. The extent of this runs deep enough that neither gender finds anything wrong with it, and thus make no effort to change it.

**Authority**

When the first Nesoren and Kynodus decided to create one unified tribe, they decided to create a ruling family by marrying the Nesoren chieftain and Kynodus princess, as well as the taking of a few concubines. The resulting lineage (what would eventually be called the [[Audaunis|Audaunis Lineage]]) is the line of chieftains within the tribe, and possess the ultimate authority. A [[Cipher]] is used to make a marking on all firstborn of the family, which physically signify their claim. 

In addition, men of high positions within each race also gained authority, their lineage forming a council of advisors to the chieftain.

## Unification of Duske


As the population grew, so did their power and strength. While they were originally only a coastal tribe, they expanded far and wide as their population grew, eventually taking over most of the islands in the Isles. [[Etharii]] were sent to [[Exedis Sanctum|Exedis Sanctums]] in exchange for certain conditions, boosting their might considerably. Many Daunis support the military might of their nation, alongside the Kynodus. And, as they kept advancing, it soon became necessary to expand to the surrounding mainland.

The Forgotten Isles, now known as Audaunis: the Seat of Dawn, began sending expeditions and eventually delegations. And what they found their was a place torn apart by the force of Zenelith itself. If left unchecked, the calamity could worsen and eventually pose a major problem to Audaunis itself.

It was, after much debate, decided to push back the Etherion calamaties, bringing the aid of [[Wardchain]] [[Lynx|Lynxes]] to seal the mountains temporarily. The first prince himself, Audaunis [[Harthkyn the Herald]] said to be the most skilled warrior in Audaunis and second coming of [[Kynod the Sun|Kynod]], led the war effort personally, uniting the tribes under one banner.

Eventually, after years, Duske was purged of the rampaging akashraal, reducing the concentration of Etherion and lightening up the weather. The land was still harsh, but it was habitable. To those who spent decades suffering under Duske's hell, it was akin to the [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms]] themselves. 

Many considered Kynordith a hero, willing to follow him. Due to the vast differences between Duske and Audaunis, it was decided to make Duske into its own nation, while Audaunis will become a vassal state.
