The Great Sundering was the great war fought between the [[Gods]] in both the [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms|Greater]] and [[Lesser Realms]] almost five million years ago during the [[Timeline|Age of Fire]], far before the advent of [[Logi]].  The reason for the war, along with the participants (as it was not just the Gods) is unknown. As is the reason the Gods were able to move beyond their prison and why [[ORDER]] did not intervene.
