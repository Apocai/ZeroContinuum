Logi are an umbrella term for sapient beings such as [[Panhumanity|panhumanity]] and [[Manah]]. They vary vastly in appearance, language, culture, etc. 

Logi is plural, Logoi is singular. The term men, while originally used for panhumans (more specifically humans), now applies to all Logi.
