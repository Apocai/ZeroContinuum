The Greater Realms are the metaphysical side of the Universe, mirroring the [[Lesser Realms]].  

Unlike the Lesser Realms, the Greater Realms are not divided by the cosmos, and have no concept of space. 

It primarily exists to serve as ORDER's prison for the [[Gods]], leaving them unable to wreak havoc in the Lesser Realms.

The landscape itself varies greatly, from floating islands to empty voids to infinite seas. It is a fantastical, mystical, place, though it has no real biological ecosystem. 

The Greater Realms are also the source of Etherion found throughout [[Creation]]. It acts as a well upon which Etherion flows down to the Lesser Realms.

**Relationship between Realms**

Note that the Lesser Realms are not inferior to the Greater Realms. The primary purpose of the Greater Realms is to regulate and control the Gods and direct the flow of Etherion throughout [[Creation]]. While the [[Ais]] that inhabit Greater Realms are revered through many mythologies and religions in the Lesser Realms, they too are mortal. The massive amounts of Etherion has boosted them well past the classification of mortal creatures, but they can still die.

The naming for the Realms comes from how Etherion is produced from the Greater, and trickles down to the Lesser. Further information can be seen in [[Etherion]]. 

In addition, unlike the Gods, the Ais can move between the Greater and Lesser Realms without restriction. Most tend to stay in the Greater, but some have also wandered off down to the Lesser Realms, where they are weakened from lower Etherion density.

**Ecology**:
The Greater Realms do have their own civilizations, though they differ greatly from the Lesser Realms. Many are based around a central figure (usually a God), and tend to stick to themselves. 

Food is only necessary for beings possessing less Etherion - the more powerful can survive entirely off of Etherion (though not as easily as [[Nerathys]]). 

Without facing threats such as food or water shortage (most threats being Gods, for which there is no protection against), civilizations tend to be less technologically advanced than the Lesser Realms.

More on [[Ais]]

**Inhabitants**:
Other than the Ais, many strange creatures are also found in the Greater. They come from a variety of origins: some are descendants of animals that have come from the Lesser, some where spawned by the will of Gods, etc.
