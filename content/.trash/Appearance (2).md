## **Appearance**

Arin's beauty is heralded far and wide across Duske. Her blonde hair is akin to golden silk,  cascading down her back like a waterfall. Her eyes are the Audaunis gold, a remnant of her bestial heritage, while her ears are slightly pointed in lieu of her [[Nesoren]] ancestors. From her proud face, to the regal cheekbones and cherry-red lips, Arin has often been said to look like a noble wolf, with a primal, almost terrifying beauty. 

![[Arin 1.png| right lp| 300]]
She looks to be in her late twenties, with a tall, lithe physique bringing to mind the lithe, graceful physique of the Kynodus.

Her form is accented by feminine curves, with a prodigious bust and a straight, well-sculpted back that flowed down into the expansive shelf of her rear, from which flow down her long, muscled legs. 

##### Clothing

Arin, as the Titan, wears a patterned, ceremonial breastplate crafted out of [[Bittergold|bittergold]], along with similarly patterned vambraces and leggings, both made out of the skin of an [[Ushul]]. The breastplate sacrifices defense for comfort and appearance, shown through its slim, form-fitting appearance.

What stands out is a golden gauntlet, worn on Arin's right hand. It looks more like a glove, and is intricately patterned with the words of an old tongue, saying 'A fist to quell the earth'. 

This is the **Titan's Grasp**, and is the symbol of a Titan's station, functioning akin to a crown. 

Worn over the armor is a long white cloak-like cape, painted with the image of a half-sun.
