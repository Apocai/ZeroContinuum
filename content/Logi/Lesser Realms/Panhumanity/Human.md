Humans are a species of [[Panhumanity|Panhuman]] (in fact being their origin). They hold their roots in [[Zenelith]], were they descended from the [[Ais]] of the defeated of [[The Bleeding King]] during the [[Great Sundering]]. 

Over the next few eons, they went to work spreading across Zenelith, as well as some through different worlds - the method of how they managed to get there before the invention of [[Fluxgate|Fluxgates]] is a befuddling question, though it has been theorized that this is due to space-time fractures caused by the [[Kuzarian Hammerblow]] during the Sundering. 

##### Society

Their society is vast and multicultural, especially with the inclusion of other [[Logi]]. They are considered quite unique due to affinity to [[Etherion]], a remnant of their ancestors' divinity. Due to this, before [[Timeline#^2ccc71|Etherius]], they were shunned and ostracized by other sapient races (though not to the extent of the Omnikris), owing to their high rates of [[Etharii]]. 

Still, following Etherius (and due to [[Thoran-Zul]] dominance), humans began advancing the quickest to their many [[Lynx]].
