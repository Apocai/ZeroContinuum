The Heretical Physique is the body type gained from undergoing an extremely abusive conditioning method created by [[Thoran-Zul]], as a way for humans to increase their limited physical abilities to beyond those of other [[Panhumanity|Panhumans]]. 

Initiated during [[Timeline#^2ccc71|Etherius]], the program was ultimately abandoned after roughly a decade due to a severe lack of viable results. The conditioning regimen was brutal and cyclic: subjects were forced to train to the point of complete muscle destruction, heavily doped on Medium [[RED|RED's]] to accelerate unnatural recovery, and then subjected to the process all over again.

While the program yielded a fraction of success—producing subjects with entirely new muscle groups and drastically heightened strength—the resulting bodies were unstable and anatomically incomplete. Ultimately, the subjects' internal organs were incapable of supporting their hyper-accelerated biology, inevitably leading to systemic organ failure and death.

#### Appearance

All who underwent the program only held incomplete physiques, so this description will be pertaining a completed one based on [[Apocanyser|Apocanyser's]] (only known holder) body.
