## Overview

The Nesoren are a group of [[Panhumanity|Panhumans]] that migrated from a distant land to the [[Saestan Islands]], a little over two thousand years ago. They are characterized by their lean physique, pointed ears, bronze skin, and heavily sun-bleached ashen hair. Most interesting are the white patterns across their body. At first, it may seem like tattoos, but is actually a hereditary [[Curse]] put on their clan in antiquity. The reason for the curse itself is unknown, and the effects of the curse have been almost completely negated through generations of labor.

They have lifespans ranging from 250 to 320 years. 

**Clothing**

The men and women both used to go topless, and are dress in loincloths. As their civilization, [[Seastan Islands|Audaunis]], progressed, they have gained many technological advancements reflected in their society and architecture. In the case of clothing, technology has led to strange but fashionable trend of a hybrid of modern and antique clothing.

They are a sea-faring clan, though they are bounded close to the coastline, lest they drift off too far into the [[Dark Ocean]], from which there is no return.
