Ortherium is a naturally forming ore create when residue left by decayed Etherion reacts with underground minerals. As such, ore deposits are typically located in areas of high Etherion density.

Ortherium is highly prized by Zenelithi nations due to its capability to create [[Bright]], a highly adaptable energy source and material. As such many off-world colonies under the nations are mined for their Bright. 

The ore glows a bright, neon pink.
