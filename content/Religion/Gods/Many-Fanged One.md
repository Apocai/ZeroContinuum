The Many-Fanged One is a mysterious [[Gods|God]] widely reviled and feared across [[Creation]]. The reason for this is said to date back untold eons ago, as the very biology of creatures (both of the [[Lesser Realms|Lesser Realms]] and [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms]]) trembles for its name and influence. Even the Gods, as mighty as they are, also seem to display unease at its mentioning. 

It is not referred to by its [[Gods#^de9d95|Udra]], as it is said that name is so accursed it has been forcibly removed from history. 

#### Onikris

The [[Onikris]] (themselves having descended from the [[Magnakris]]), are the very distantly related descendants of the Many-Fanged One, and as such have been persecuted throughout their history. 

Despite having recently been established as [[Panhumanity|Panhumans]] rather than [[Akashraal]], they still face wide discrimination. Many nations on [[Zenelith]] outright refuse them entry to their lands, and a few others actively raid their villages for culling or enslavement. 

#### Beasts

^be6b8b
The Many-Fanged One possesses no [[Aiasin]], as it never breeds. It's existence is so strong that it has no need of Ais - even if lesser beings forget its name, their fear of it is ingrained into their very being. They are characterized especially by having four arms or arm-like appendages - no more, no less. While the concept of Beasts are now no more than myth, it is widely known all Beasts have four arms. 

Instead, its children are called Beasts. They do not have to be directly related to the Many-Fanged One, but all are imbued with a fragment of it. They are old creatures, dating back to  [[Timeline#^faf709|Umbrus]] and before.

The Beasts are said to be the Harbingers of the End Times, and are walking catastrophes. Almost all have been sealed away during Umbrus, by the now deceased ancient civilizations, and a few more have even been slain. Yet a few still remain free, either slumbering or hidden from the world.
