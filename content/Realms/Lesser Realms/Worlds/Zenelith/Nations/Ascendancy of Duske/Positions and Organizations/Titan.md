The Titan is a central figure of power within the [[Ascendancy of Duske]], second only to the [[Ascendant]]. They are the head of the [[High Court]], and for the most part oversee the nation. They defer to the Ascendent as well as the High Court, in the case of majority rule. Their power is such that they are above the [[Lorde|Lordes]], though not nearly enough to fully bring them to heel. 

Their symbol of office is a right hand gauntlet named the **Titan's Grasp**, enscribed with the inscription 'A fist to quell the land'.  ^7410b1

The gauntlet's origins seem to date back to the [[Nesoren]], where they kept such a gauntlet safe in hopes of gifting it to their "chosen king". It was first given to [[Audaunis Arinthor]]
