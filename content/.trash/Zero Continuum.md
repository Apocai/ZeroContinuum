The Zero Continuum is an (hopefully) extensive cosmology, built formerly last October, but with ideas since middle school. It is very much a work in progress, so empty notes, typos, etc. are common. 

If you notice anything you would like to add or do, message me. This can be regarding the characters' powers, building nations, planets, Gods, etc. I do not mind any suggestions, as it helps keep the Zero Continuum unique and fresh.

If you have any critiques, either on the writing, the characters, names, etc. please tell me. I would rather you be harsh so I can fix my work than lie and make my work remain bad. 

(Warning) The work has many sexual elements in it, ranging from descriptions, clothing, customs, rituals, etc. I have tried not to make them overshadow the rest of the Zero Continuum, but they are still a large part of it. If you feel any such elements take away from the work as a whole, please tell me.

##### Navigation

There are many terms here, and it will be easy to become lost. I will recommend a few notes to start with, after which you will gain enough familiarity to navigate through the rest.

On the left, you will see folders. Inside each folder will be either notes, or more folders. They are categorized by subject, so it will be easy to find anything you are looking for. 

Much like Wikipedia, there are hyperlinks that will take you to specific notes. Click on them if you want to know more. 

- Anyway, start with [[Universe]], and then work through the Greater Systems and Principles folder. 
- Read [[Etherion]] and the Etherion folder
- Read [[Lesser Realms]] and [[Greater Realms]]
- Read [[Zenelith]]
- Then, go to the [[Timeline]], and read there. 
- Then read in whichever order you please
- In order to have the easiest time understanding, click the hyperlinks as you go through the notes.
