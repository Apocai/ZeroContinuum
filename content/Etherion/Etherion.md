Etherion is the name given to the mysterious substance that serves as the ultimate building block of [[Creation]]. It is from Etherion from which all things are built on, even the laws of physics. It is the driving force of Creation, and the ultimate energy source. 

Etherion, as of [[Timeline#^e9b1ac|Dominus]], has yet to be explained by science. It's essence is not fully understood even by its wielders, the [[Lynx|Lynxes]].

It is heavily influenced by the [[Gods]], who could be likened to Etherion incarnate. They are intrinsically connected to it, through both their nature, and due to the Gods' prison, the [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms]], being the origin from which Etherion "trickles" down to the [[Lesser Realms]].

Indeed, such a fact implies Etherion does not exist everywhere at all times, but rather flows. Despite the questions it may raise, as Etherion is the underlying layer of Creation, that is indeed the case. This is explained due to the connection the Greater and Lesser Realms share. They are two sides of Creation, and are linked as such. While areas of the Lesser Realms may be more or less dense with Etherion, the Greater Realms shares uniform density everywhere. With such being the case, as long as the corresponding area of the Greater Realm holds Etherion, the Lesser Realms will operate as normal. As a World's [[World Factor]] increases, this metaphysical border between the two realms weakens, allowing more Etherion to flow through, and thus making that area in the Lesser Realms more dense. 

***
 Etherion decays, which prompts the necessity of replenishing it (much like applying a fresh coat of paint). This replenishing is done constantly by the Etherion coming from the Greater Realms. Even still, there are still areas that are more or less Etherion dense, such as [[World Factor|Worlds]]. In such places, Etherion passively gathers, attracted by the World. 

When Etherion "decays", it does not get destroyed, but rather seems to slowly vanish, as though it is being pulled somewhere. Such a discovery has been made during [[Timeline#^2ccc71|Etherius]], has led to debate about the existence of a **Third Realm**, below the Lesser Realms. There have been no results to such a query, though research still continues. 

The decaying of Etherion leaves behind a certain residue, which seeps into nearby minerals to create [[Ortherium]], which is used to create a major energy source known as [[Bright]].  

#### Gates

All living organisms will begin circulating Etherion passively when exposed to it. However, that doesn't mean it is good for them. Some creatures, such as the [[Manah]] of Zenelith, found Etherion to be highly poisonous and as a result went extinct. 

However, to those who do benefit from them (namely Akashraal and panhumans), Etherion is a boon. Etherion is circulated by organisms through **Gates**, areas of the body where the soul and physical body are most deeply connected. These vary in number from being to being, even among those of the same species and race; that said, they stay roughly around the same the number by species/race. 

It is through these Gates that Etherion circulates throughout the body, enhancing it. The effect isn't immediate, but the over generations it becomes quite noticeable. 

The size of Gates are also important, though that doesn't necessarily means bigger is better. Just like water through an opening, small Gates means Etherion can be circulated much more intensely and with more focus, resulting in more power and speed at the cost of lower output. On the other hand, larger Gates correlates to more output, with lower power and speed. The size of Gates are not dependent on genetics, but training: they can be changed to be bigger or smaller, often rapidly. 

However,  Gates can get clogged, especially though bodily wear, Etherion overuse, or mental tension. As such, especially among those with high-stress occupations and activities, it is necessary to periodically flush them through a process known as [[Gate#^2af867|Catharsis]]. 

#### Technology

Etherion is a dangerous substance, and cannot be directly manipulated. This is due to it being influenced by the Gods, along with their madness. As such, direct contact by an ordinary human often leads to brain damage, various forms of madness, and even death. 

For most of [[Logi]] history, Etherion is something only to be touched and used by [[Lynx|Lynxes]] and no one else. For they alone are [[Chain|Chained]] to the Gods, granting them control over it.

With the Age of Etherion, heralded by [[Zul-Irinth-Kazyn]], the advancement of Lynxes in turn fueled research into how to tap into Etherion, an energy source unrivaled amongst all others. 

Such research brought fruit, resulting in a way for even the average man to manipulate Etherion - the [[Switchrod|switchrod]]. Using this tool, men could harness it to make many new appliances and technologies, such as the [[Etherlink]], [[Gazer|gazer]], and of course, many military adjacent weaponry.     

Even so, Etherion is highly volatile. While incredibly rare, it has the potential to become corrupted, either through misuse, reactions, or other accidents. The exact nature of the corruption is unknown and varies based on what is being corrupted, but it could lead to data becoming unreadable and madness inducing or even inanimate objects becoming "malignant". There have been records of such cases across the history of Etherion use, but they have dwindled with new research and leaps in science.
