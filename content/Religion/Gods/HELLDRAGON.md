Not much is known about the Helldragon, only that it is feared even among other [[Gods]], and is despised by the [[Tyrant of Nine Dominations]] and its [[Tyrant's Aiasin|Aiasin]], both for stealing away its [[Godmaiden]] [[Daemonium Theana]], and for an unknown event in the far past that resulted in the Helldragon stealing the name of "dragon", from the progenitor of dragons, the Tyrant. 

The Helldragon is not actually a dragon, but a Wyrm, though what a Wyrm is is unknown. The same goes for the Helldragon's true form, as its only noted appearance is what appears to be a massive black sun, slumbering deep below the [[Umbral Chasm]].

At times, it sends its Incarnation [[Avarasin]] out into the world to journey and explore. 

The Helldragon (more specifically Avarasin) has taken in both [[Nerathys]] and [[Apocanyser]], bringing them to the Umbral Sanctum as children ad=nd giving them to the care of the first of her [[Demon Kings|Demon Kings]], Theana. She has also fathered a [[Demigod]] with Theana, named [[Serana]].
