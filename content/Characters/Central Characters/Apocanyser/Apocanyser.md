##  **Overview:**

Apocanyser is the ward of his guardian and mentor [[Daemonium Theana]] and brother of [[Nerathys]] and [[Serana]]. He is a soulless being, and hosts an artificial soul named the [[Warden]] within himself.

Apocanyser is currently affiliated with Duske, where he lives as the Servant of [[Lunthiel]]. He spends his days training, looking after (read: playing) with Lunthiel, and going on missions for [[Audaunis Arin|Arin]]. 

 Apocanyser is the developer of the [[Executioners|Executioner]] Program, and thus, consequently, was given the moniker of the 0th Executioner: the Extinctioner. Such a title is not given without meaning: during one mission Apocanyser killed accidentally, and subsequently went on a murdering spree until he was incapacitated.  

His identity as the Extinctioner is kept a secret, even from other Executioners. Thus, he remains to them simply as the Servant of Lunthiel.



## **Background**

There was once a young boy living in a village surrounded by forest in the distant corners of [[Zenelith]]. His people had experienced much hardship, due to both an abnormal increase in [[Akashraal]] and strange shifts in weather. One night, he had snuck out, determined to find a special type of herb that could cure his loved one's illness, going against village decree.

As expected, the boy was attacked by Akashraal, becoming badly wounded. He had, by some miracle, managed to flee, moving deeper and deeper into the woods until he came across an expansive field, littered with the corpses of the beasts. Though that was the end of his fortune, as he succumbed to his wounds and died.

In the center of the plain stood a _thing_ covered to the brink in blood, organs, and tissue. It bore several deep wounds across its body, though no blood poured out. Regardless, it was clear it would not live long.

Yet, in its final moments, it saw a corpse, the corpse of a human. Not only that, but it still had attached to its soul, though it would soon detach and return to the [[Gwinvarga]]. 

The thing staggered towards the corpse, and, in its final moments, devoured it, trying to meld the boy's soul to its body, operating on some mindless directive to survive.

But it was too late, as the soul had already departed. And all that was consumed was the soulless corpse.

Thus the thing died, and its corpse was all that remained. But, after a few days, _something_ burst out of it. It looked to be a mirror image of the boy, yet was clearly not. There was no specific reason, yet one could tell at a glance there was something unnatural about him, a certain hollowness that no living creature would possess. And he would be named Apocanyser.

Apocanyser had only fragmented memories, memories of the boy. Yet, using those memories he tried returning to the village.

Throughout the entire time he was hunted and killed repeatedly by Akashraal. Only, he would regenerate. No matter how much of him was crushed, snapped, sliced, or eviscerated, any and all wounds would heal almost instantly. Of course, he still felt pain, and was at times tormented for horrors by Akashraal would harm him without ceasing, all the while the boy healed instantly.

Still, he made it back into the village in his(?) memories, though the process took a month. But of course, the villagers response was what was expected. They bore witness to a missing boy covered in blood and scars, yet without a single wound. While their first reaction was to care for him, they noticed some things: How he could not speak or understand their language, how his eyes had changed from a lovely gold to a dull silver, and most importantly, _questioned how he had survived that long in the forest_.

Eventually, the villagers came to a conclusion: he was not the boy they once new, but something else that had taken his appearance and memories. Even his family was forced to acknowledge that, as Apocanyser was clearly different from the boy of their memories. Still, they couldn't bear do any harm to the child, beloved by the village, instead decided to cast him out.

And so he was driven from the village, given only a small knife and a pack of supplies. Of course, the Akashraal attacks came once more, tearing and mutilating Apocanyser's body, sometimes for days on end. Yet he would not die, for any damage was healed instantly.

Eventually, he was saved, by the wandering, [[Avarasin]], the [[Helldragon|Helldragon's]] [[Gods#^455431|Incarnation]]. He was clothed and bathed, and was taught language. He travelled with her as she explored, before she took him to her [[Domains|Domain]], the [[Umbral Chasm]] where he was placed under the care of her First [[Demon Kings|Demon King]] [[Daemonium Theana]]. 

Apocanyser spent much of his youth in the Chasm, within the [[White Citadel]], the [[Holds|Hold]] of Theana. He lived with her, along with her daughter [[Serana]] and adoptive daughter [[Nerathys]].

Much of his early days were spent adapting to his body. Much like a newborn, Apocai lacked any time to adapt to his body, and spent years flailing around before gaining control over his body.

Eventually, after he was deemed ready to do so, he began to be trained by Theana, along with his siblings and the [[Acolytes]]. However, he was far and away the weakest.

To remedy this, Apocai was trained far longer and harsher than the others, though he was grateful for it. He progressed somewhat, but was still unable to compete with the greatest of the Acolytes or his siblings. He trained relentlessly for years, though compared to others he grew relatively little, and remained unable to bridge the gap between them.

It was Theana who first noticed something wrong with him. His regeneration was unheard of course, but it should have been easy enough to explain as the effects of an imbued [[Chain]] or [[Cipher]]. Yet this, coupled with the odd feeling she would get when watching Apocanyser made her conclude something she never thought could be possible: that he lacked a soul.

It was an outrageous statement, as [[LAW]]. dictated that the neither the soul nor body can exist without the other. To break such a thing would be ascend beyond logic, and become a [[Gods|God]]. Yet Apocanyser clearly was not, nor was there a sign [[ORDER]] was even aware of him.

But somehow, it was true. Apocanyser has no soul. He was like a doll, an empty shell. And yet he possesses an ego, along with thoughts and feelings, however dampened. He had all the characteristics of a soul, yet did not have one. Of course, such a thing did not lead to any difference in treatment. Being who she was, completely oblivious to any tact, she pulled him aside one day and explained that he had no soul.

It was something Apocanyser had already realized, from how he felt so hollow when looking at the vibrancy of others' lives, especially Nerathys who seemed to shine (albeit with what he thought was a sinister glow).

Still, Apocanyser wasn't dissuaded from training, and continued just as vigorously.

And it was just a day like any other, when it happened. A cataclysmic rift had formed within the Chasm, leading to the Lesser Realms. This was no attack, but rather the product of the slow entropy of Creation, having lasted past its appointed time. It caused many casualties among the cities of the various Demon Kings, including, unfortunately, Apocai.

Apocanyser crashed into Zenelith, in the [[Andanaht Mannes]], a vast, ancient land said to be the Twilight of Man. He was forced to live his days running and fighting all manners of hellish creatures to survive. Of course, he was immortal, but the dangers of being feasted on eternally, cursed, being trapped, etc. still remained.

Apocanyser spent countless years here, killing and being killed in a never-ending loop. His body aged, going from looking in his teens to late teens or early twenties, though regrettably not much in height.

He ventured further and further, uncovering various mysteries and secrets in Andanaht Mannes. As he desperately struggled his way through this hell, he uncovered the underground ruins of a bygone civilization. While most of it was annihilated, he found a hidden, highly-advanced library of immense size, containing not books but strange, autonomous mechanical dummies.

After further research, he concluded that these dummies were designed for sparring, and were miniature "books" that would hold combat techniques. These ranged from all manner of combat, from weapons to bare-handed. The civilization seemed to have spent much of its time learning and training its soldiers in the combat styles of others, though that didn't save them from whatever disaster wiped them out. Apocanyser, believing it to be a boon, began to dig right in.

The dummies patrolled the library and surrounding ruins, attacking anything on sight. Due to a malfunction, there was no sparring to practice and train; it was a battle to the death. Apocanyser would face the attacks head on, letting it attack his body, before healing and beginning to fight in earnest. He would spend his time fighting the dummies, and then further practicing what he learned on different dummies and Akashraal.

These dummies were all set to the highest skill-level, making it so Apocanyser had to take months each to fully uncover and learn their skillset. Even then, Apocanyser was unable to use many of their skills to the best effect, instead changing it to fit his own.

It was impossible, at least for him, to master every dummy's skills separately. Due to the vast amount of dummies, Apocai began to mash and meld the learned skills to create his own preferred combat style. He forewent proper martial arts, having no need for grapples and locks, instead focusing on bringing out the fullest amount of offense possible, at the detriment of his defense.

And after almost a century, Apocai had completely "read" the entirety of the library. Though much of the information was lost to his conscious self, and was reflected subconsciously through his fighting.

Eventually, Apocai arrived at the southwestern most point of Andanaht Mannes, at the [[Taryld Duske]], before collapsing from exhaustion. He was caught by a patrol of Dusken soldiers from the neighboring [[Ascendancy of Duske]], who brought him to a secure containment cell. Most panhumans are incapable of surviving within the Mannes, and thus Apocanyser was regarded warily.

## **Appearance**

Apocanyser is a young man who looks to be in his late teens to early twenties. He has a lean, wolfish face, with sharp, aristocratic features. He has been described by others as looking haughty and arrogant, though his personality says otherwise (at least outwardly). He has tired, steel grey eyes, and is clean shaven, with bronze skin and jet-black hair streaked with strands of grey. 

He has a stocky frame, though he is quite muscular. He has several new muscle groups, concentrated in the back, core, arms, and legs, curtesy of developing the Heretical Physique. Such a form has often been told to look unnatural, though it said by a few to possess an alien beauty. 

##### Clothing:

He is normally dressed in the finery of the Dusken elite, more often than not favoring dark, somber colors. His clothing is normally full sleeved and full-legged, so as to avoid showing his physique. 

His armor is an older model of the basic Executioner [[Executioners#^3359c0|Sheath]], one that comprises on defense for increased mobility. 

It is full-body, though  the material is lighter and thinner than standard models. It is a uniform grey, with the half sun of Duske emblazoned on the breast. The helm fully convers the head and is faceless, and is made of the same grey material.  


## **Personality**

##### Outer

Apocanyser is a very deliberate and unhurried individual; while not methodical, his pace often comes across as lazy. He carries an attitude of general dispassion that manifests as being deeply unmotivated about most things, which others frequently misinterpret as unfriendly or even brooding. This isn't to say he is completely inept in socializing, merely that he doesn't care for it. Whatever happens outside is of no concern to him, so long as it does not involve him. After all, the battle Apocanyser faces was only ever against himself.

Despite his overall unconcern for others, he still takes notice of how he is perceived by them. However, this is less about their opinions and more a reflection of his own self-perception, extending only to his physical appearance; what thoughts lie beneath his surface bear no relevance to him. Yet, there is a kindness to him. It is a kindness rooted not in warmth, but rather in honor—he will help others almost like an obligation. It is less to actually aid them and more to fulfill his own code.

Ultimately, others have said there is a numbness to the way he lives, as though he is not truly present in the moment, but detached from everything but himself. It is as if he is watching the world through a screen—able to appreciate what he sees, but never truly _in_ it. This is his own variance of solipsism: a state where Apocanyser acknowledges the lives of others, but has absolutely no interest in them.

#####  Inner

The child was never happy. There was nothing that captivated him, nothing that interested him, and nothing that made his desire living. At the time, his sole reason for existence was to not make his Theana's efforts caring for him go to waste. 

Even as he adapted to his soulless body, this did not change. The young boy failed to see any beauty in life, and wondered for what reason he existed. And that was when he met Nerathys.

Through her, he saw a blinding light that was the polar opposite of him. He understood her to be similarly a blank state, yet she fully embraced her emptiness as a way to absorb everything. The boy tried emulating her, only to realize he couldn't share her passion. And, noticing such a laughable sight, she told him: 

*Fufufu. How pathetic you are, brother. dearest It is so simple, and yet that brain of yours couldn't think of it. Fine then. I shall tell you, for the sole reason that it will haunt your life forever: 

*If you lack passion, then steal it. If you wish for vibrancy, than drain such color from others. After all, this world was built upon such laws, no?*

At the time he couldn't understand what she was saying, yet her words resonated within him. Having no clear agency or goal, he continued along the path of strength, hoping the meaning of her words would become clear to him.

So he trained for that reason alone. So that he may find the answer to his conflict. So that he may fill his hollow self. But of course, such peace is never easily obtained. 

So what must he do? How could he succeed? He struggled for years with this. He grew hateful and envious, feeling only jealousy at the talent and skill of others. It tore at him, until he grew to despise himself, him and his weakness. Try as he might, he could not overcome the divide, the gap that divided the strong from the weak. 

He pushed himself, but there was only so far he could go before his mind and body collapses. Yet he continued, to the reprimanding of Theana. She had told him he had begun to train only to punish himself rather than improve, though to Apocanyser those were one in the same. 

One day, as he finally managed to take his first kill in the training grounds, an emotion arose not guilt or joy, but a clarity. It was as though he had lived with a fog clouding his mind, leaving behind an enlightened state. He felt he could do anything, be anything, kill anything. He was no longer burdened by his weakness, and could finally be free. 

The moment faded, and Apocanyser was left haunted. That was no possession, no alter ego. The thoughts he had, they were his alone, akin to arriving at the solution to a long attempted problem. And the minute he snapped back to reality, the solution went with it. In that instant, he had truly wanted to kill everything he saw, whether it be his fellow students, sisters, or mother. Which was his true self, the man he believed himself to be, or the man he became after murder?

He thought to himself: to act in that state would be to surrender, to become a slave to himself, and that is the one bit of freedom that he will never let be taken away. Subconsciously, rules were hammered into his being, a code of honor he himself cannot navigate. And foremost of them was:

*He will not kill, because to kill is to forsake himself*.

But was that really right, or was he merely running away?

##### True Inner:

^1556c1
At his core, a question would plague him. Bound as he was by his morals and rules, was he not already a slave? How could he yearn to have freedom from  his struggles, to ascend beyond on them, if he was not willing to break the bonds that bound him?

It is only through murder that he briefly arrives, no, becomes the answer, because through murder he achieves **Oneness of Mind and Body**. His entire being is concentrated on one act: to kill. Hesitation, doubt, agony - any and all such conflict is wiped clean, leaving behind only the singular moment of purpose. 

Nerathys's words were proven here. She told him to steal the passions of others, yet it only became clear in this singular instance. 

Realizing he is a weak being, a man who needs to fuel his own ego to truly live, he will pit himself against the all of existence, using their deaths to prove himself the stronger, the worthier, and kindle his ego. That was the purpose he arrived at, and what he, in the instant her arrives at his oneness, seeks out to do. Everything he has built, everything he has created goes back to that one realization. Even his code was but an offshoot of this. He felt his desires, his honor, not as a shield to protect against his bloodthirst, but as yet another means of source of kindling. In the depths of his psyche, he would impose not a rule, but a challenge - to follow that rule, to take the harder path was to prove his strength, while to forgo it be yet another reminder of his weakness. 

## **Abilities**

#####  Regeneration:
Apocanyser possesses abnormal regeneration that seems almost like a reversal of time rather than regeneration of organic material. But why?

He possesses no soul, and yet his physical body still acts like he does. Such a thing goes against [[LAW]], which states the the body and soul exist in tandem. In other words, when one is injured, so is the other. However, as Apocanyser does not possess a soul, when he is harmed, and no soul is similarly harmed, that must mean he wasn't harmed. Thus, his wounds will heal instantly, as though to correct any impossibilities. The process of healing however, is agonizing, and exacts a mental toll on Apocanyser. He can only go through so many regenerations before he loses control of his muscles and flops limply onto the floor, a state which takes days to recover.

##### Oversoul:
Oversoul is Apocanyser's favored way of fighting difficult enemies, and is a way of enhancing his physical and cognitive abilities using the assistance of [[Warden]].

As Apocanyser is soulless, he uses Warden as a substitute soul (see more in [[Warden]]). When he needs to dramatically boost his abilities, Warden "pushes" her soul outwards, clashing against Apocanyser's physical body and threatening to overcome it. However, LAW dictates that the body and soul are one, and thus such a thing shouldn't be possible. Therefore, Apocanyser's body is reinforced to accommodate the soul pushing. The greater the soul push, the greater the reinforcement. 

Such a thing still causes damage to Apocai's body, as can be seen with the tears through his skin through which ghostly white flame flickers when using Oversoul. As such, his regeneration to heal him, which in turn accelerates his timer towards muscle control loss. Therefore, both the duration used and degree of the strength of the soul push need to be properly monitored and controlled.

##### Gates:

Apocanyser possesses 6 [[Gate|Gates]].  However, as he lacks a soul, these Gates are unable to be utilized by himself, thus necessitating Warden to operate as his soul. 

Apocanyser has worked heavily on strengthening his Gates alongside Warden, though at present he has yet to unlock their full potential. Still, the Gates greatly enhance his cognitive and physical abilities (though most of the cognitive enhancements are used by Warden).

##### Pain Tolerance:
Due to Apocanyser's heavy use of his regeneration and Oversoul, he can ignore most pain. There are times when some level of pain gets through, but such is the price to pay for power. 

##### Heretical Physique:
See more in [[Heretical Physique]] 

The Heretical Physique is a body structure created by the repeated doping of [[RED|RED's]] whilst training hellishly. 

Apocanyser's healing (which is many times stronger than an Ultra RED) and his destructive training has naturally created a perfected Heretical Physique. 

Due to being natively enhanced by Etherion (like all other denizens of Zenelith), his body is already superhuman; yet this body boosts him even further. Apocanyser has gained greatly increased physical abilities, as well as greater control over his body due to the creation of new muscle groups. Perhaps most important is that his muscle density made him heavier, close to almost 500 pounds. 

However, his reflexes and senses, while heightened, are not nearly to the extent of his body, and as such are things he trains in earnest. 

Alongside this is another drawback of the Heretical Physique - reduced flexibility. As such, Apocanyser diligently trains everyday to improve this, yet it still remains at the level of an ordinary person. 

##### Fighting:
Apocanyser's fighting style heavily relies on his regeneration. He is always on a ceaseless offensive: blocking weapon attacks with his body, losing an arm to close the distance, etc.

His lack of talent is one of many sources of frustration for Apocai. His talent itself is average, but compared to the many geniuses around him it may as well have been nothing. His skill in martial arts and weaponry pale to that of a true master, though he will nevertheless strive to match them. 

His fighting style itself is one crafted from the brutalized and perverted form of techniques he has acquired. He can use most weapons, though he only has proficient skill in them, at least when compared to masters like Nerathys. He tries to use weapons to increase his proficiency, though as a result he tends to ignore actually defeating his opponents and instead treats it as training.

Still, he is much more adept at fighting bare-handed, though he is even less skilled with martial arts. Why? Martial arts were created as a way for humans to use skill and technique to combat other humans. For beings like Apocai, martial arts only serve as dead weight. 

##### Oneness of Mind and Body

Oneness of Mind and Body is the inner peace achieved only through murder. It is assumed to be a mindless, trance-like state that seeks only to kill - whether they be friend or foe. In such a state, [[Warden]] is suppressed, thus making Apocanyser's Gates unusable. In addition, his regenerative abilities are also nullified, making him incredibly fragile. In such a state however, he has no concern for his own survival. It is a challenge, a pitting of the soulless against a soul. If there was no drawbacks, how could he hope sate his ego? 

In exchange for fragility, Apocanyser becomes much more proficient in killing, unburdened as he is by thoughts and desire. The fruits of his labor, his diligent training, are shown through this, with every movement focused to but one purpose - murder. And as he continues to kill, so to does this prowess grow.
