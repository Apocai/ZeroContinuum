The Executioners are the [[Ascendancy of Duske|Ascendancy of Duske's]] strongest force and their most renowned fighters. The Executioners are seen more as myth than an actual group, and their origins, strength, and missions are all obscured to the public eye. 

The Executioner Program was created by [[Apocanyser]], and modeled after his own experience in the [[Andanaht Mannes]]. Indeed, many of the Executioners ended up becoming stronger than him, showing that it was indeed a success. 

Worth noting that Executioners are not Lynxes, as the mutated brains all Lynxes have is incompatible with the Executioner enhancements. 

#### Overview

The Executioners are the elite task force (numbering 103) in service of the[[Lunthiel| Ascendant of Duske]], though [[Audaunis Arin|Titan Arin]] retains active command. They are separate from the military, and act exclusively through the wishes of the Ascendant, normally for assassination, extermination of [[Akashraal]], exploration, etc. 

They thrive most heavily in combat, though each Executioner is specialized for different functions. All have immense, superhuman capabilities, as well as an advanced cognition, various cybernetic implants. Beyond this, they are equipped with a customizable armor known as a **Sheath** as well as a similarly customizable weapon called a **Blade**.  

The Executioners use [[Arsenal]], their all-in-one command terminal AI, to receive orders from the Titan, upgrade/commission gear, exchange and swap out weapons, gather information, etc. 

They are allowed free-roam anywhere within Duske, but are headquartered in a secret facility underneath the [[Duske Spire]] called the [[Haven]]. Within the Haven is an unique series of [[Fluxgate|Fluxgates]] known as the **Nexus** that are connected to various colonies of Duske, as well as unexplored worlds and potential colonies. This is the backbone behind their fearsome reputation, how they are able to appear almost anywhere with ease.

##### Origin (The Program)

The Executioners are all graduates, or rather, survivors of the **Executioner Program**, a deadly, 12 year training with a fatality rate of near 95%.

The recruitment starts at a young age, normally around 8-10 years old. Executioners will look through the streets, orphanages, and various other places where the dregs of society gather and offer them a choice: to have the smallest potential of changing their life forever, or remain in their current situation. They are made aware of the dangers of the Program, but around 1/3 end up taking the offer. 

They are brought to secure labs within the Haven and are administered various drugs and giving physical and mental conditioning to prepare them for the Program. The drugs (or more accurately poisons) cause severe nausea, pain, and fever, and can lead to excessive bleeding, blindness, and many other symptoms. However, those who survived have gone being mere man, and have gained heightened awareness, cognition, and physical abilities. Those who couldn't make it or gave up are instead given an antidote to cleanse the poison, before having their mind wiped and given to the care of well-regarded orphanages within [[Laitskaer]]. Most end up dropping out, with only around 10% of the children succeeding and willing to proceed.

And then begins the Program. 

The children, called **Aspirants** are sent to a secure base on the border of the [[Andanaht Mannes]], where they will spend the next 4 years training with senior Executioners, who will train them in a variety of combat styles, including many dedicated towards battling Akashraal. 

After this, they will be sent lose within the Andanaht Mannes, where they will be expected to survive the next 8 years, upon which they will become full-fledged Executioners.

To aid in their survival, the location of various outposts will be shared, stocked with basic medicines and first aid, as well as a months worth of provisions. Cooperation is highly encouraged, but not necessary. However, the act of killing a fellow Aspirant is punishable by death. The Aspirants will be dropped of at outposts randomly, and are prevented from escaping the Mannes through [[Ward|wards]]. 

Before being dropped off, they are given one last chance to opt out. If they chose so, they will be given a position within the Haven, either as personnel or instructors. 

At the conclusion of the Program, they are reforged, the crucible having burned away all weakness from their flesh. Yet one task remains, one that would turn them from man to something more. To complete their initiation, each Aspirant consumes a drop of blood from the Ascendant, the larval [[Beast]]. Indeed, everything up till now was to train their mind and flesh to be strong enough to absorb the Beast blood and rise again as Executioners. Most tend to pass, though the process of having the body acclimate to the blood is painful. Yet with that, they become full-fledged Executioners. 

Upon the conclusion of the Program, the new Executioners are given 6 months of leave, before they are directed to return to the Haven. It is here that they will receive their Sheath, as well as given access to the Arsenal. Furthermore, they will begin their training, not only in combat now but in academics, history, politics, government, etc. This is to prepare them for the many tasks that they will be given, which encompass a broad area. 

##### Sheath and Blade

^3359c0

The main driving force behind the Executioners is their **Sheath**, which gives most of their additional abilities.

Upon first receiving their Sheath, it is a blank slate. It's appearance is that of an armor of dark alloy, coupled with a faceless helm. It also comes with a rod made of the same material- the **Blade**. 

The Sheath drastically improves physical abilities and defense, yet its true abilities lie in versatility. That is, it can be constantly upgraded and remade by Arsenal. Likewise with the Blade.

But with what? The bodies of Akashraal. The Akashraal have unique bodies cultivated from a mix of Etherion and biology. Their body parts are a wonder of nature, and boast incredible properties, all of which could find many a use in society. Yet people across the [[Lesser Realms]] have tried and failed in finding a method to harvest the Akashraal, due to their inability to overcome the rampant Etherion that destabilizes the Akashraal's corpse upon their death, leaving behind only ashen remains. 

However, Duske was able to overcome this through the Executioners - their Beast blood allows them to stabilize the Etherion as they fight, allowing for the retrieval of the corpse. 

When an Executioner slays an Akashraal, and wishes to bring it back, it be transported to the Haven, where they either have it stripped for part (which will be used to upgrade their Blade/Sheath), or sold for a currency known as **lune**.

Every Akashraal offers unique abilities, which can greatly strengthen the Executioner's own, through their Sheath and Blade. This can range from offensive to defensive, single target to area, healing, etc. It is generally accepted the stronger the Akashraal, the greater the ability. 

##### Arsenal (Box)

An Executioner doesn't just receive a Sheath and Blade upon initiation; they also receive a sub-terminal of the Arsenal. This terminal serves as a 4th-dimensional "box", that stores all their Sheath and Blade upgrades, amongst other things, which can be taken out and applied to the Executioner's equipment at any time. 

The Arsenal travels with the Executioner at all times, and appears as a floating ring behind their back. It can be camouflaged, but will deactivate it during usage. 


##### Hunting

Executioners however, are not able to simply seek out and hunt Akashraal whenever they want, due to the environmental backlash and safety of the Executioner. Instead, they are designated to hunt only when verifying it is a threat to Logoi populations or to the ecosystem itself. Beyond this, they may also hunt when given a mission to do so, or if it interferes with their mission. 

##### Missions

Arsenal functions not only as central command, but also as a quest board of sorts. 

Arsenal will receive a mission or request from Lunthiel or Arin, and then will assign it to the most fitting candidate for the job. A corresponding lune reward is also offered, alongside **merit**.

Missions can range from exploring untamed regions, covert operations, assassination, etc. 

Typically, only one Executioner is sent at a time, as there are rarely any cases were more than one would be overkill.

##### Lune:

Lune is a currency used within the Haven to regulate the management and spending of Sheath/Blade repair, buying of Akashraal parts, and other things by the Executioners. It is gained through the completing of missions, and increases based on the difficulty of the mission (based on the decision of Arsenal). 

##### Merit:

Merit is a system of fame that is received upon every successful mission, and increases based on the difficulty of the mission. 

Merit is what determines the quantity and difficulty of the missions assigned by the Arsenal. 

##### Reputation

The Executioners are feared throughout some parts of [[Zenelith]], and are even known on some none-Duske colonies in the [[Lesser Realms]]. The reason is simple: they are clouded in mystery.

As the greatest secret of Duske, they are kept so well hidden that they ar regarded as a myth. 

While their strength is matched by other similar groups of a few other nations, what isn't is their adaptability, of being able to swap out entire combat styles in seconds. 

It is said that when a lone man approaches an army, the army should cower, for the only one able to do such a thing is an Executioner.
