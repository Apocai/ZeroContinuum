The Abominations, also known as the Stareaters, Omega Beasts, or Monsters of the Endtimes are the strongest lifeforms within the [[Lesser Realms]]. 

They are not the same as [[Gods]]. Omegas inhabit the Lesser Realms and, despite being living beings, they possess no souls, an anomaly not known to anyone as of yet.

They exist in many different places in the Lesser Realms. Usually they are slumbering or in a comatose-like state, though some wander. Their existence is not widely known, often kept a secret for all but the highest authorities. 

While they vary in appearance, all are immense in size, ranging from the size of cities to the size of planets. Their very nature and biology is alien to humans. Unlike the Gods, the Omega Beings are living creatures, though they are near impossible to kill. 

Note that the only Abominations that exist in inhabited celestial bodies are those in a deep sleep - no living thing can survive near such a monster. As such, awakened Omegas typically reside on dead planets, or float through space.

**Origin**:
They have many different origins: some are transformed [[World Factor|World Factors]], while others came into being following cosmic events (i.e. an Etherion-fueled supernova). They are theorized to be the offspring of the [[Universe]] (not [[Creation]]) itself, though this is but a theory.

They are more like an naturally formed machine than a biological creature. Thus, they need fuel in order to sustain themselves. This fuel is typically gained from ultra-concentrated Etherion, found mostly commonly in stars (hence the name Suneater). 

**Power**:
An Abomination, from their birth, are beings without equal. Their individual powers differ, but all are capable of destroying entire galaxies with little effort. 

**Intelligence:**
Their intelligence is unknown, they could be mindless or sentient. 

They are completely immortal, not through their mortal souls, but rather their bodies. Being able to regenerate from their very atoms, immune to non-conceptual attacks, and being able to revive themselves endlessly. Among all things, they alone are the ones who survive to the Big Freeze, after which they must be wiped out by [[ORDER]] itself. 

**Relationship with UKNOWN**

The [[UNKNOWN]] prey upon the vast majority of the Lesser Realms (and sometimes even beings from the Greater Realms), but the Omegas are beings that are beyond that. Of course, such battles have never been witnessed (or at least no one has returned alive from seeing it), but it is interesting to theorize.
