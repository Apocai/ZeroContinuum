Akashraal are a collective term for creatures born of Creation itself. They are found both within the [[Lesser Realms]] and [[Greater Realms]].  

In the Lesser Realms, they begin to form once a world's [[World Factor]] becomes high enough (<= 1). They will then start materializing, drawing upon Etherion to create an organic body. As such, they have a higher chance of forming in regions of high Etherion density, making such places incredibly hazardous. Stronger and stronger Akashraal begin forming as the WF increase. 

Furthermore, as Akashraal are formed from Etherion, they may also be influenced by the [[Gods]] (as the Gods of tainted the Etherion with their influence). In such cases, their species depends on what God's taint manages to imbue itself onto the Akashraal. For instance, the [[Tyrant of Nine Dominations|Tyrant's]] taint would cause the Akashraal to manifest as a [[Dragon|dragon]]. Further influences in the region's Etherion will then further influence the Akashraal, giving it unique characteristics.  Akashraal that have manifested through a specific God's influence are called **Divine Akashraal** and are considered children of that God, much like the [[Ais]]. As such, they can naturally form and wield a [[Chain]] with their Divine Father. Divine Akashraal are a rarity, and are spoken of with awe and fear.

Akashraal are considered reasonably rare by societal standards, even on a high WF world like [[Zenelith]]. Most tend to inhabit Etherion-dense regions, which [[Logi]] tend to avoid.

#### Biology

Akashraal are similar to [[Etharii]] in terms of bodily function. They require sustenance, however that may be found, as well as sleep. They do not defecate, as like Etharii all their waste turns to liquid decayed Etherion, and is urinated or perspired. 

Akashraal, like animals, breed and reproduce. They typically have offspring once every few years to decades, as Akashraal are long-lived creatures (ranging from a century to thousands of years).

Divine Akashraal, due to their divinity, fall outside these biological rules. Many are immortal, or have lifespans in tens of thousands of years. They reproduce very rarely, and possess greater intelligence than their common brethren.  ^fba576

###### Spawning 

Typically, Akashraal will be formed from Etherion when a world's World Factor increases a level, after which they will turn to biological means to reproduce.

What Akashraal will form depends on the specific quality and density of Etherion in a region. Variants can also occur if the Etherion in that reason fluctuates (due to [[Pulse Storm|pulse storms]] for instance).

New Akashraal formed after a WF shift are typically stronger variants of the previous Akashraal, provided the Etherion quality and density hasn't changed in that particular location. 

##### Classification

Akashraal are classified according to their general appearance and physiology.

**Primary Classification:**

Chitari - Insectoid or crustacean Akashraal that use exoskeletons.

Brute - Powerfully built Akashraal that rely on physical dominance. They are muscular and are quite resistant to normal attacks.

Reaper - As opposed to Brutes, these Akashraal are ambush and pack predators that rely on speed and precision over strength. They are normally headed by a singular Apex (see secondary classification).

Wyvern - Primarily airborne Akashraal that possess wings or adjacent limbs. These primarily inhabit the skies and seas. 

Serpent - While including serpentine Akashraal, the Serpent classification is based on the long, sinuous body shape rather than reptilian characteristics. As such, these Akashraal vary heavily in attack strategies, ranging from ambush predators to active chasers. However, almost all belonging to this class are solitary. 

Phantom - The most elusive and rare type of Akashraal. This classification is a broad name for all visually elusive Akashraal, including those that might be incorporeal.  

**Secondary Classification**-

Abyssal - a unique classification given to specialized Akashraal who feed off of [[Pulse Storm|pulse storms]]. They are typically found within the [[Dark Ocean]]. 

Apex - these are typically variants of common Akashraal found, including Reaper pack leaders. They are stronger than their common counterparts, and require improvised strategies to defeat them. 

Colossus - Immense in size and cannot be killed without severe loses and destruction; only applicable strategies is for their redirection. 

Armored - Akashraal with unusually strong armor. These Akashraal require extreme offense to penetrate and are near invincible without the right tools or strategy. 

Ruiner - Akashraal who alter the environment itself to better secure themselves, typically at the detriment of other beings. They require unique approaches as the battlefield itself becomes a hazard.
