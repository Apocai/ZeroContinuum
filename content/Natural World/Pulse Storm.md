A pulse storm is a storm caused by unstable Etherion, which collides and reacts with the surroundings to produce extreme weather conditions. It occurs most often at sea, and is dreaded by all who travel it. 

In addition to freak weather, pulse storms also disrupt Etherion technology and can even react with a living being's natural Etherion absorption, causing them to go berserk or become die from overload. 

It is near impossible to survive through a pulse storm, though they are able to be predicted by observing the native Etherion in the area. The one exception to this rule is within the [[Dark Ocean]], where the naturally unstable Etherion ensures pulse store come without warning. 

Akashraal too are victims of pulse storms, though most flee before the storm arrives. Again, the one exception lies in the Dark Ocean, where there have been reports of Akashraal able to not only survive such storms, but grow stronger through them.
