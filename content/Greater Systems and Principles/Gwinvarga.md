The Gwinvarga is the system that stores and reincarnates souls.

There is a fixed pool of souls from outside of time that is tapped into whenever a new life is birthed. Once dead, souls are sent to the end of time (to the Big Freeze), whereupon they will reincarnate as beings of [[Lost Paradise]]. When they die there, their souls are sent once again to the end of that cycle (the Big Bang), where they will reincarnate as beings of [[Creation]].

The most confusing concept here is that Lost Paradise and Creation don't exist in tandem, but sequentially. Time functions more as a loop than a single strip. Souls can immediately travel from the beginning to the end of time, and vice versa, which is how souls can be steadily reincarnated without decrease in overall population. 

Normally, when one grows closer to death through bodily wounds, old age, etc., their soul is similarly reduced. And eventually, upon their death, the soul is extinguished. This extinguished soul is then sent back to the Gwinvarga, where it is then reincarnated. 

[[Gods]] of the [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms]], however have immortal souls and are unable to die. Instead, they exist until the end of time, upon which they will have their souls devoured to birth the Gods of Lost Paradise. The souls are not lost, but instead are devoured by the new Gods and endlessly consumed by them until the end of Lost Paradise, where they are then sent to Creation. 

Due to the disrupted [[Continuum Infinus]], souls are instead sent to the unformed Lost Paradise, where  they become creatures that exist in a state of neither life nor death, waiting for Creation to end. Eventually, there will be more no more souls in the cycle, and the population of Creation will eventually dwindle to nothing.
