The Andanaht Mannes is a vast, unexplored region in [[Zenelith]], within [[Varithyl]]. It borders the [[Ascendancy of Duske]].

It is an incredibly dangerous area, though there are only legends of what lies beyond. The land is obscured from visions by vast swahts of fog - from what could be seen the Andanaht looks to be covered in strange, unsettling flora.

Most explorers who set foot into the land rarely make it back; those who do report a massive inland sea, bizarre weather conditions, and [[Akashraal]] the likes of which have never been seen before. Etherionic technology malfunctions within the area, leaving explorers reliant on older tools that use electricity and nuclear power.
