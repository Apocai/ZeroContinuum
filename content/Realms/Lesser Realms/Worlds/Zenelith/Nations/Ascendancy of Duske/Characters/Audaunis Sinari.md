### Lore

Sinari is the child of [[Rancor]] and an unnamed [[Panhumanity|Panhuman]] woman. The woman was brought to the [[Manigrym]] tribe, yet died soon after giving birth to Sinari. The baby displayed almost none of the traits of an [[Magnakris]], and thus it was decided to quietly drop him off in front of the an orphanage in [[Laitskaer]], where he would be amongst those more similar to him.  

He grew up here, though his appearance steadily changed as he grew older. Subtle tracings of fur grew along his body, while his body became far more physically strong than the average [[Panhumanity|Panhuman]], moreso that even other [[Onikris]]. After being crowned the Champion of the annual [[Tourney of the Titan]], he was given one wish by Arin. He wished to become her squire and learn from her, eventually yearning to become a member of the [[Fist]]. 

Once Sinari grew to manhood and started awakening to his latent powers, he was approached by the Manigrym, who claim he would lead them. He was made aware of his heritage, to which he soon approached Arin with. As the Manigrym would provide to be staunch allies and are incredible fighters, Arin decided to delegate with them on how to best proceed - resulting in Sinari becoming her Lorde Consort. 

### Appearance

Sinari is a handsome man looking to be in his mid teens. He has shoulder-length silver hair and a youthful, finely featured face. He has a lean, athletic physique. Evidence of his heritage is seen his canine ears, sharp incisors, and fur alongside his spine, upper back, and thighs. 

### Personality

Sinari has a hedonistic approach to life, motivated by his blessed life. His appearance and natural prowess in the arts of combat have allowed his life to, while not be completely smooth, be carefree. 

Such a life has also made him quite arrogant, believing himself to be above the common people. As such, he has a habit on looking down on others he believes not worthy of his attention, and tries to surround himself by only does he acknowledges - this is not merely status, but a lieu of things including talent, intelligence, and ability.

Sinari is also quite fond of the opposite sex. His good looks and boyish demeanor, coupled with the knowledge on how to use his innocent appearance has allowed has allowed him to seduce many, often older women.

Nevertheless, aside form his lust, Sinari does display more positive traits. He is fiercely loyal and proud of his nation, and holds Arin in high esteem (one of the reasons why he was attracted to her). He also trains frequently to keep up his skills, though Apocanyser notes that he relies quite a bit on his inborne abilities. 

### Abilities

Sinari is the son of [[Rancor]], a [[Akashraal#^fba576|Divine Akashraal]] and ancestor of the Manigrym. As such, his heritage grants him a wealth of power, including heightened physical abilities and stamina. 

Sinari, being half Akashraal, has 8 Gates, and frequently trains in [[Gate#^2af867|Catharsis]] and [[Gate#^6b8dcf|Crucis]] to improve their circulation. 

Sinari is also able to transform into a Manigrym. He can do so at well, though it may also erupt uncontrollably during periods of heightened emotion. In this state, his physical abilities increase tremendously, and his Gate circulation almost doubles. Unlike other Manigrym, he has pure silver fur during this state, and is noticeable smaller than other males, standing at a height with shorter females. Despite this, he is still considered one of the strongest Manigrym, and deeply respected by them. 

### Relationships

##### Arin:

For Sinari, it was love at first sight. From the moment he laid eyes on her, he became infatuated, and wished to one day make her his. Perhaps it started as a simple boyhood crush. But unlike others it never felt. He loved everything about her, from her appearance to her martial prowess to her diligence and her love for her country. Even her reputation as the Iron Monarch and a merciless queen only served to spur him on. It can even be said that all his training and effort was to win her attention, and eventually her heart. 

When his heritage was revealed by the Manigrym, and Arin made the decision to take him as her Lorde Consort, Sinari wasted no time to begin laying with her. While his passion was reciprocated in bed, Arin did so methodologically. Even if she skillfully performed whatever was needed, she did so almost clinically. Sinari noticed that even if her body reacted to him, Arin's face itself would remain cold save for a lightly flushed cheeks. It wasn't that she reviled him, but just didn't hold much romantic interest in him. Still, he realized that she at the very least didn't mind their nighttime activities, and may even find it pleasant, and so was satisfied.   

However, even though Sinari enjoyed Arin quite a bit, he frequented other women as well, especially the noblewomen of Duske. Arin for her part allowed it, so long as his seductions helped manipulate and keep tabs on them.

###### Manigrym:
Arin, by forging a [[Chain]] with Rancor, was accepted into the Manigrym tribe as their Priestess. Yet with that also came her involvement in their sexual customs, including providing ritual [[Gate#^2af867|Catharsis]] and proving her dominance and strength through  [[Gate#^6b8dcf|Crucis]] with their warriors. 

Sinari is normally quite possessive of Arin, believing her to be a flower only he could pluck. As he had yearned after her for so long, he on some level wanted Arin to return his desire. As she didn't, he conceded it fine so long as she did not show another man more interest.  
So when, Arin was to be the Maingrym's Night Priestess, a figure within their society who would regularly perform ritual Catharsis and Crucis when needed, Sinari felt stuck. He did eventually relent, as he realized the Manigrym did not consider such acts to be infidelity or obscene. That, and Arin's assurance that to her such practices were only considered further training.
