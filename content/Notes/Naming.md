

Below is a rough draft for the names and naming of various things in the [[Zero Continuum]].

This section will contain names

As I am not skilled nor motivated enough to create a new language, most of my names are created for either for the sole purpose of sounding cool (mostly character names) or are translated from dead languages meaning certain things (i.e. [[Andanaht Mannes]] means Twilight of Man in a combination of Old English and Gothic). While I do not focus on a specific language (so as to make Null seem more diverse and multicultural, most names are derived from a combination Latin, Ancient Greek, Norse, Gothic, Old English, Welsh, and Sanskrit, among others. 

So anyway, here it is:
(names are loosely grouped alphabetically)

**[[Apocanyser]]**- out of universe, the name is pulled from Apocalypse, and made when I was kid. The name sounds stupid, but its been used long enough that I cannot change it without making a different character. **[[Seastan Islands|Audaunis]]**- name roughly means "Of the Sun". 


**[[Ascendancy of Duske|Duske]]** - perversion of an old name for the Mountains of Duske called Dultharask, meaning Evil Father. 
**[[Daunis]]** - Taken from Audaunis.

[[Ral-Varsha]] - translates to Greater Gate. The 'Ral' prefix can either mean Greater or Lesser, depending on whether it is put before or after a word.
