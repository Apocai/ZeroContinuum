The Acolytes are [[Daemonium Theana|Daemonium Theana's]] students, handpicked up at a young age from various places in the [[Umbral Chasm]] for their talent to serve as forces of the Helldragon, either by older Acolytes or the Demon Kings. They have been trained by Theana herself for many centuries, each becoming masters of combat.

Upon reaching a certain level, they become [[Sentinels]] and are sent to the various [[Demon Kings|Demon Kings]], where they serve under them and receive additional, more personal training. They are cycled through each Demon King, receiving different trainings, before being assigned a location to be stationed at based on their preference. 

**Theana**
They are given the same cold and aloof treatment that Theana treats all others. A smile, except on rare occasions is almost never seen, and her arrogance is such that it makes interacting with her difficult. However, she treats them all fairly, never hesitating to instruct them or help them improve. She provides them a home, strength, and respect... occasionally.

Both her power and her character has won her the admiration and fear of all acolytes. They act with the utmost respect possible towards her at all times, and always treat her with formality, calling her master or mistress.

##### **Apocanyser**

They were brought after [[Apocanyser]]'s adoption, and are secretly jealous of their mistress's  favor towards him, though they will never say this out loud out of respect. 

While the Acolytes consider each other brothers, the thought never extended to Apocanyser due to his differences. They trained together, ate together, and slept together while Apocanyser mostly kept to himself and was trained personally by Theana alone due to his condition. Of course, that wasn't to say they harbored him any ill intent at this time, merely that they didn't interact much to form a bond. 

Prior to his return to the [[Umbral Chasm]], they looked down on him unconsciously for his weakness, especially considering his status as Theana's most treasured student and her unofficial son. 

Upon his return, while at first he seemed somewhat stronger, his willingness to completely ignore defense and ignore harm to his body to attack repulsed them.

Still, they acknowledged his strength, though neither party desired to form friendship. However, all who sparred with him noticed there was something missing when fighting him. 

##### **Serana**

They are much more intimate and familiar with Serana, as she regularly spars and coaches them despite being younger. The Acolytes consider her their foster sister, forging a close bond.

Serana, even as they grew up, regularly bathed and slept with them, something that has made them all the more aware of her beauty. Like with Theana, they were entranced by her, and secretly fought behind her back for her hand. Of course, they were much too timid to actually approach their Mistress's daughter, and could only watch.

##### Nerathys

It is her they fear above all else. She trained among the Acolytes before her descent to the [[Lesser Realms]], and was regarded unquestionably their better in every form. From academics to sparring, there was nothing they could do against her. But if it was only that, they would at the most feel awe and respect. 

[[Nerathys]] terrorized them, day in and day out, solely for her amusement. Whether it be coercing, bullying, mocking, or bringing them to an inch within death out on the sparring grounds, she had her way with them. Theana turned a blind eye as well, not out of favoritism, but because she thought that was merely the privilege of the strong. She believed such torment would do well in making them strive for greater heights, and she was not wrong. 

Still, even after her leave, Acolytes regularly remind newcomers to run at the first sight of her, and to "Fear the Ghoul".
