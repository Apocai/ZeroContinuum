Creation refers to the [[Universe]] when it is reigned over by [[ORDER]]. 

Creation is home to many forms of life, from sentient to animalistic, mortal to divine, etc. However, almost all are confined to their host worlds and nearby celestial objects - straying too far into space risks exposure to the [[UNKNOWN]]. Due to this, all forms of space travel are close to impossible.

Creation is divided into the physical, mortal [[Lesser Realms]], and the divine, metaphysical [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms]]. 

These Realms together maintain balance in Creation, and as such, is under the judgement of Order, until the [[Continuum Infinus]] dictates his time over, and Creation falls for the rise of [[Lost Paradise]].

***
There is only one timeline within Creation, as every other possible timeline is pruned by ORDER. Note that this does not mean an absence of free will. There is still the freedom to choose what action to take, just that any "wrong" choices will lead to the timeline being culled. Therefore, every thought, choice, and decision made within the Zero Continuum is exactly how ORDER wishes it. 

Ordinarily [[Nerathys]], with her timelooping ability, would be culled by ORDER. However, her abilities are protected from its eyes by [[DOMINION]], who exists outside time.
