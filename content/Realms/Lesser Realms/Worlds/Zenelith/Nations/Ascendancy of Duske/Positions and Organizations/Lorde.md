A Lorde is the highest ranking noble below the [[Titan]], and of whom form the [[High Court]] that governs the [[Ascendancy of Duske]]. They are the leaders of their [[Vael|Vaels]] and are given land with which they govern. 

Their titles and positions goes back centuries ago, back to before the unification of Duske. Most are the descendants of tribe leaders, while some others became so through great achievement, while others are branch families of the [[Audaunis|Audaunis]] Royal Line.

Lordes follow many of the sexual customs among the Audaunis and other Lorde families. Mostly, it is done through their Gatherings, large banquets held throughout the year for the Audaunis, Lordes, and their families to socialize and discuss politics. 

**Gathering**

During Gatherings, the wives and daughters of Lorde's are expected to dress in revealing outfits that are nevertheless befitting of their status. For example, this may include a beautiful ballgown that is cut at the at the sides to show the gentle curve of the hips, or a very deep neckline to flaunt the breasts.

The purpose of this is for the Lordes to present their women, their sexual appeal being seen as a sign of status and power. Other may freely admire the women, though that is all.

The Titan (if female) or Titan's wife is expected to dress more conservatively than the other women, to highlight the difference in status. Unlike the Vaels, they are of the Audaunis, and should be treated as higher.
