
There was once a young boy living in a village surrounded by forest in the distant corners of [[Zenelith]]. His people had experienced much hardship, due to both an abnormal increase in [[Akashraal|Akashraal]] and strange shifts in weather. One night, he had snuck out, determined to find a special type of herb that could cure his loved one's illness, going against village decree.

As expected, the boy was attacked by Akashraal, becoming badly wounded. He had, by some miracle, managed to flee, moving deeper and deeper into the woods until he came across an expansive field, littered with the corpses of the beasts. Though that was the end of his fortune, as he succumbed to his wounds and died.



In the center of the plain stood a *thing* covered to the brink in blood, organs, and tissue. It bore several deep wounds across its body, though no blood poured out. Regardless, it was clear it would not live long. 

Yet, in its final moments, it saw a corpse, the corpse of a human. Not only that, but it still had attached to its soul, though it would soon detach and return to the [[Gwinvarga]]. 

The thing staggered towards the corpse, and, in its final moments, devoured it, trying to meld the boy's soul to its body, operating on some mindless directive to survive. 

But it was too late, as the soul had already departed. And all that was consumed was the soulless corpse.

***
Thus the thing died, and its corpse was all that remained. But, after a few days, *something* burst out of it. It looked to be a mirror image of the boy, yet was clearly not. There was no specific reason, yet one could tell at a glance there was something unnatural about him, a certain hollowness that no living creature would possess. And he would be named Apocanyser. 

Apocanyser had only fragmented memories, memories of the boy. Yet, using those memories he tried returning to the village. 

Throughout the entire time he was hunted and killed repeatedly by Akashraal. Only, he would regenerate. No matter how much of him was crushed, snapped, sliced, or eviscerated, any and all wounds would heal almost instantly. Of course, he still felt pain, and was at times tormented for horrors by Akashraal would harm him without ceasing, all the while the boy healed instantly. 

Still, he made it back into the village in his(?) memories, though the process took a month. But of course, the villagers response was what was expected. They bore witness to a missing boy covered in blood and scars, yet without a single wound. While their first reaction was to care for him, they noticed some things: How he could not speak or understand their language, how his eyes had changed from a lovely gold to a dull silver, and most importantly, *questioned how he had survived that long in the forest*.

Eventually, the villagers came to a conclusion: he was not the boy they once new, but something else that had taken his appearance and memories. Even his family was forced to acknowledge that, as Apocanyser was clearly different from the boy of their memories. Still, they couldn't bear do any harm to the child, beloved by the village, instead decided to cast him out. 

And so he was driven from the village, given only a small knife and a pack of supplies. Of course, the Akashraal attacks came once more, tearing and mutilating Apocanyser's body, sometimes for days on end. Yet he would not die, for any damage was healed instantly. 

Eventually, he was saved, by the wandering [[Avarasin]], the [[Helldragon|Helldragon's]] [[Gods#^cb5013|Incarnation]]. He was clothed and bathed, and was taught language. He travelled with her as she explored, before she took him to her [[Domains|Domain]], the [[Umbral Chasm]], where he was placed under the care of her First [[Demon Kings|Demon King]] [[Daemonium Theana]].

***
Apocanyser spent much of his youth in the [[Umbral Chasm]], within the [[White Citadel]], [[Holds|Hold]] of Theana. He lived with her, as well as his two sisters [[Nerathys]] and [[Serana]] that came to live within the Citadel.

Much of his early days were spent adapting to his body. Much like a newborn, Apocai lacked any time to adapt to his body, and spent years flailing around before gaining control over his body. 

Eventually, after he was deemed ready to do so, he began to be trained by Theana, along with his siblings and the [[Acolytes]]. However, he was far and away the weakest. 

To remedy this, Apocai was trained far longer and harsher than the others, though he was grateful for it. He progressed somewhat, but was still unable to compete with the greatest of the Acolytes or his siblings. He trained relentlessly for years, though compared to others he grew relatively little, and remained unable to bridge the gap between them. 

***
It was Theana who first noticed something wrong with him. His regeneration was unheard of course, but it should have been easy enough to explain as the effects of an imbued [[Chain]] or [[Cipher]]. Yet this, coupled with the odd feeling she would get when watching Apocanyser made her conclude something she never thought could be possible: that he lacked a soul.

It was an outrageous statement, as [[LAW]] dictated that the neither the soul nor body can exist without the other. To break such a thing would be ascend beyond logic, and become a [[Gods|God]]. Yet Apocanyser clearly was not, nor was there a sign [[ORDER]] was even aware of him. 

But somehow, it was true. Apocanyser has no soul. He was like a doll, an empty shell. And yet he possesses an ego, along with thoughts and feelings, however dampened. He had all the characteristics of a soul, yet did not have one. Of course, such a thing did not lead to any difference in treatment. Being who she was, completely oblivious to any tact, she pulled him aside one day and explained that he had no soul. 

It was something Apocanyser had already realized, from how he felt so hollow when looking at the vibrancy of others' lives, especially Nerathys who seemed to shine (albeit with what he thought was a sinister glow).

Still, Apocanyser wasn't dissuaded from training, and continued just as vigorously.

***
And it was just a day like any other, when it happened. A cataclysmic rift had formed within the Chasm, leading to the Lesser Realms. This was no attack, but rather the product of the slow entropy of Creation, having lasted past its appointed time. It caused many casualties among the cities of the various Demon Kings, including, unfortunately, Apocai. 

Apocanyser crashed into Zenelith, in the [[Andanaht Mannes]], a vast, ancient land said to be the Twilight of Man. He was forced to live his days running and fighting all manners of hellish creatures to survive. Of course, he was immortal, but the dangers of being feasted on eternally, cursed, being trapped, etc. still remained. 

Apocanyser spent countless years here, killing and being killed in a never-ending loop. His body aged, going from looking in his teens to late teens or early twenties, though regrettably not much in height. 

He ventured further and further, uncovering various mysteries and secrets in Andanaht Mannes. As he desperately struggled his way through this hell, he uncovered the underground ruins of a bygone civilization. While most of it was annihilated, he found a hidden, highly-advanced library of immense size, containing not books but strange, autonomous mechanical dummies. 

After further research, he concluded that these dummies were designed for sparring, and were miniature "books" that would hold combat techniques. These ranged from all manner of combat, from weapons to bare-handed. The civilization seemed to have spent much of its time learning and training its soldiers in the combat styles of others, though that didn't save them from whatever disaster wiped them out. Apocanyser, believing it to be a boon, began to dig right in. 

The dummies patrolled the library and surrounding ruins, attacking anything on sight. Due to a malfunction, there was no sparring to practice and train; it was a battle to the death. Apocanyser would face the attacks head on, letting it attack his body, before healing and beginning to fight in earnest. He would spend his time fighting the dummies, and then further practicing what he learned on different dummies and Akashraal. 

These dummies were all set to the highest skill-level, making it so Apocanyser had to take months each to fully uncover and learn their skillset. Even then, Apocanyser was unable to use many of their skills to the best effect, instead changing it to fit his own. 

It was impossible, at least for him, to master every dummy's skills separately. Due to the vast amount of dummies, Apocai began to mash and meld the  learned skills to create his own preferred combat style. He forewent proper martial arts, having no need for grapples and locks, instead focusing on bringing out the fullest amount of offense possible, at the detriment of his defense. 

And after almost a century, Apocai had completely "read" the entirety of the library. Though much of the information was lost to his conscious self, and was reflected subconsciously through his fighting. 

***
Eventually, Apocai arrived at the southwestern most point of Andanaht Mannes, at the [[Taryld Duske]] of the [[Ascendancy of Duske]]. Here, he was caught by a patrol of Dusken soldiers, who retrained him with Etherion before bringing him to a hidden containment cell. No Logoi are able to survive within the Mannes, and thus Apocanyser was not identified as a human, but some manner of creature. 

Such a thing peaked the interest of [[Lunthiel|Lunthiel the Ascendant]], who personally visited him in secret. She would talk to him after his interrogations and experiments, eventually deciding to bring him along as her personal servant (though this was mostly done because she was bored). She forced him to swear an [[Oath]] to obey her every word, before bringing him to the [[Inner Spire]], her living quarters within the [[Duske Spire]]. 

Of course, there was staunch refusal from the [[Titan]], [[Audaunis Arin]]. She saw him as a threat and an unknown, who couldn't be trusted with a being as important as Lunthiel. However, she was forced to bow down before the Ascendent's will, and grudgingly accepted him. 

For Apocanyser's part, he was quite interested in the new world. He was also a man who enjoyed luxury, and thus greatly appreciated having a proper bath and bed to sleep in, not to mention food. 

Apocanyser (more for his own gain than anything else) began training Lunthiel. Lunthiel quickly surpassed Apocai with her raw physical ability and talent. As Lunthiel (despite Arin's annoyance) trusted Apocai more and more, he became privy to Duske's inner matters, eventually even being sent on missions by Arin for various reasons (he despise these, as most are grunt work).
