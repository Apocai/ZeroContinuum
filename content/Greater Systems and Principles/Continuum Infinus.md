The Continuum Infinus is the cycle the [[Universe]] goes through, from its birth with the Big Bang to its death in the Big Freeze. 

Once the Universe dies, its ruler changes from [[ORDER]] to [[CHAOS]], with the Universe going from [[Creation]] to [[Lost Paradise]]. The [[UNKNOWN]] become the new [[Gods]] of the Universe.

However, when the Big Freeze took place in the previous cycle, ORDER, rather than give up control, started a new cycle of Creation, believing the Lost Paradise was not necessary to maintain balance. CHAOS was forced back into slumber, and ORDER once again reigned over the Universe. 

Over time, this led to growing corruption. The [[Gwinvarga]] was thrown out of balance and the UKNOWN began filtering into outer space.
