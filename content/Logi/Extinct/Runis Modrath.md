The Runis Modrath are an unknown, extinct race of [[Panhumanity|panhumanity]] that were active in the previous iteration of [[Creation]]. 

They are beings shrouded in mystery, and no records of their existence has survived, even on [[Zenelith]], the world that survives through every passing of the [[Continuum Infinus]].   

The sole record of their existence lies in the culmination of their very being: [[DOMINION]], and their successor and final creation: [[Nerathys]]. 

The reason for their extinction is unknown, but according to Nerathys, it was a truth that must never be spoken of.
