Simply put, the Universe is everything. It is that which encapsulates all of space and time, and is far more than just a physical realm. It is currently under the dominion of [[ORDER]], and as such is called [[Creation]].

The Universe can be likened to a blank canvas. When a [[Paragon]] reigns over the Universe, they paint the canvas in their image (Creation or [[Lost Paradise]]). The Universe serves as the initial template from which everything is built upon, and when a Paragon's reign is over, the canvas returns to its blank state as the next Paragon paints over it.
