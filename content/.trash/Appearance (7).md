
The Warden looks to be a girl in her early to mid teens, with long, unruly white hair cascading down her  back and beautiful honey-gold eyes. Her skin is olive, glowing with a healthy tan, while her features resemble that of Nerathys.

Her body is supple and lithe, on the cusp of womanhood. Her budding chest leads to a smooth toned torso which in turn leads into a widening of the hips, giving way to a small, shapely rear and followed by lithe bronze legs. 


## Physiology

The Warden is an artifical soul, and thus has her primary inhabitance within Apocanyser, though she uses Arsenal's main terminal within the Haven as another relay point. 

## Clothing

She is clothed in rich and fine regalia, a though of course there is no need to do so. It is simply a matter of taste she received from Lunthiel. 

She wears similar style of clothing to Lunthiel, albeit one that is far more modest, revealing little to no skin. On her face is a faceless silver mask, that can be warped to expose her mouth if necessary.
