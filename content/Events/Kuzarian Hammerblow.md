The Kuzarian Hammerblow was a great explosion in [[Zenelith]] during the [[Timeline|Age of Fire]], originating in the present day [[Kuzaria]] Crater. It  was caused by the [[Helldragon|Helldragon's]] blow against [[The Bleeding King]] during the finality of the [[Great Sundering]]. 

Had the Hammerblow happened outside Zenelith, almost the entirety of the [[Lesser Realms]] would have been atomized. It is only thanks to Zenelith's unparalleled [[World Factor#^c703b2|Wul-Ral]] that world survived, though close to all life (including all civilizations) were wiped out. 

The only remaining lifeforms were the [[Ais]] of [[The Bleeding King]], who were the ancestors of modern-day humans.
