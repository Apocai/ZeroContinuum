Onikris are a race of beings distantly descended from the [[MANY-FANGED ONE]]. They are a powerful and virile people, and have spread out across Zenelith. There are many different types, sharing different characteristics and cultures. They are the descendants of the [[Magnakris]], powerful [[Akashraal]] distantly related to the [[Many-Fanged One]]. 

They have been persecuted throughout their history for their connection with the accursed MANY-FANGED ONE. 

##### Appearance:
Their appearance varies heavily, but they resemble anthropomorphic animals, retaining the head of beasts with pronounced human features. Their cultures dictate their clothing, but most are close to or in a state of nudity.

##### Power:

As noted, they are very physically capable and excellent warriors. They have superhuman strength, speed, and endurance. They are only able to form [[Chain|Chains]] with [[Beast]] and Akashraal, and are for that reason not welcomed in [[Exedis Sanctum|Exedis Sanctums]].
