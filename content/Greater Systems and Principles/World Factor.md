A World Factor is the metaphysical core of a celestial body within the [[Lesser Realms]], scaled as a number from 0-10, which refers to the density of Etherion on a body. The lower the number, the lesser the density and vice versa. 

(For simplicities sake, a celestial object with a World Factor is called a world)

It is also a tie to a world's connection to [[ORDER]] and [[Creation]] as a whole, growing stronger as the World Factor increases. Thus, a world becomes a sort of bastion of ORDER, which is why they are never threatened or attacked by the [[UNKNOWN]]. 

A World Factor develops with the progression of the planet's history, and can increase based on what civilizations reside on it, their development, and other factors. The deeper the age and history of the planet, the greater the World Factor. A World Factor cannot decrease.

A high World Factor is not necessarily a good thing. As Etherion density increases, [[Akashraal]] start emerging, not to mention strange mutations in the planet. In addition, the World Factor dictates the closeness of the planet to the [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms]], and thus the influence of the [[Gods]]. 
^081645

Most planets, once aged significantly, tend to stay at a World Factor of 7 or 8. It is difficult to grow beyond that, because due to it being difficult for a planet to last that long without it being destroyed or dying.

Note: A dead planet refers to the removal of all of it lifeforms, whether by death or migration, down to the tiniest microorganisms. When they are thus, their World Factor begins dwindling sharply down to 0.

If a planet does start extending past 8, it is in a highly-volatile state, able to erupt into a massive burst of Etherion. 

And if it manages to go past 10, something worse occurs. All the Etherion the coalesces on the planet gather in its World Factor, where it becomes fuel to birth an [[Abominations]].

##### **Origin**

A world factor is something that takes root in a celestial body as it ages. Of course, there are certain size limits (a pebble wouldn't have a world factor), but as long as Etherion can slowly condense on it, a World Factor will form. 

Worlds that have yet to birth life will have a  World Factor of up to 0.9 (0 is only for dead worlds), but are in a dangerous state as they do are not attuned enough to ORDER to be protected from the [[UNKNOWN]]. This is the reason, beyond the difficulty of life's formations, that prevent worlds from not being more numerous. 

##### **Wul-Ral**

^c703b2

It is unknown whether worlds possess sentience. However, it is true that they have a "will", or at least some form of preservation instinct. This instinct manifests through their **Wul-Ral**, or the Lesser Will.

The Lesser Will is the unconscious act of the world to preserve itself, and will do what is necessary to prevent any damage to its World Factor. The Lesser-Wills are unique in that their strength depends on the world's World Factor, and how they respond to threats vary depending on the world.

Most Wul-Rals utilize various conceptual defenses and counters. More will be stated in the description of each world. However, all share the function of erasing the concept of "death" for the world. World cannot be destroyed by ordinary, physical means.

The Wul-Ral serves as a  protective measure to protect worlds against the [[Abominations]] [[Abominations|]] and [[UNKNOWN]].
