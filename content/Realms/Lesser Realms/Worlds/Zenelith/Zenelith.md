A supermassive, flat world, situated in the center of the [[Universe]], within the [[Lesser Realms]]. Much of it remains unexplored, due to the vast dangers that inhabit it, ranging from [[Akashraal]], great curses, and other unknown horrors.

Zenelith is ripe with history, being the sole world to continue existing through the [[Continuum Infinus]]. It's name comes from an ancient language, meaning throne. As the name suggests, Zenelith means 'Throne of God', and is stronghold of [[ORDER]] or [[CHAOS]] as the cycle turns. 

Its origin, as well as its importance to the [[Paragon|Paragons]], are unknown.

It is currently home to many different cultures, nations, and organizations, some old, some new. 

It is also the birthplace of humanity, after which some were sent off to unknown worlds (how they were transported before the existence of necessary technology is unknown). 

***
Note that is is centered, not in Creation, but the Universe, meaning that is below the layer of reality known as Creation or [[Lost Paradise]]. As such, it has no location within the physical [[Lesser Realms]], though it is still considered a part of it. 

***
##### Astronomy

Despite Zenelith being a flat world that is not in outer space, it still has the day and night cycle of a planet. However, this is not due to Zenelith orbiting other celestial bodies, but something else entirely. 

There is no Sun or Moon, but rather a massive ring of thirteen orbs known as the [[Ral-Varsha]], or the Bright Lord. 

During the day, it illuminates the world as the Sun, while during the night it's light dies down to a faint glow, much like moonlight.

##### Space Travel

Due to this, space travel is not possible, which is why (even without knowing of the [[UNKNOWN]]), Zenelith was forced to rely on [[Fluxgate]] to travel. 

##### Wul-Ral 
(see [[World Factor#^c703b2|Wul-Ral for more]])

The Lesser Will of Zenelith will attack any and all threats it deems dangerous to its survival, including its own inhabitants. 

- It has the ability to create an Annihilator, powerful, mindless creatures that act as vessels of the Lesser Will, and will eat and absorb the world's enemies. Once absorbed, they will be analyzed by the Lesser Will, before taking their powers and abilities and applying its functions.
- It can bestow immense power to selected [[Avatar of the Lesser Will|Avatars]], who will then act as the sword of the Lesser Will. The Lesser Will will allow them whatever is needed to vanquish the enemy.
