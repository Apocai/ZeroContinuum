
All living organisms will begin circulating Etherion passively when exposed to it. However, that doesn't mean it is good for them. Some creatures, such as the [[Manah]] of Zenelith, found Etherion to be highly poisonous and as a result went extinct. 

However, to those who do benefit from them (namely Akashraal and panhumans), Etherion is a boon. Etherion is absorbed by organisms through **Gates**, areas of the body where the soul and physical body are most deeply connected. These vary in number from being to being, even among those of the same species and race; that said, they stay roughly around the same the number by species/race. While the locations where they reside are physical, Gates themselves are intangible. 

It is through these Gates that Etherion circulates throughout the body, subtly enhancing it. The effect isn't immediate, but the over generations it becomes quite noticeable. 

The size of Gates are also important, though that doesn't necessarily means bigger is better. Just like water through an opening, small Gates means Etherion can be circulated much more intensely and with more focus, resulting in more power and speed at the cost of lower output. On the other hand, larger Gates correlates to more output, with lower power and speed. 

Note that Gates aren't exclusively important for Lynxes. Having properly circulating Gates help non-Lynxes perform at their physical and mental peak. Their senses, reflexes, and focus heighten, and they generally become better. However, the size of their Gates don't matter much.

All beings have Gates in their head, heart and groin (in the case of some beings, it is where their approximate counterpart would be). Gates could be almost everywhere in the body, and depending on the location, can serve various purposes beyond simply increasing the amount of Etherion one can circulate, including raising precision, control, strength, etc. Even the addition of one more Gate is a tremendous boost in ability.

Having more Gates is dependent on genetics: 

- Humans have the most out of all panhumans, having at minimum 5 Gates with a rare few going up to 7. 

- [[Akashraal]] on the other hand have a minimum of 7 Gates, and some have been found with up to 10. 

- Ais, being the closest beings to the [[Gods]], have quite a bit more Gates. Most have around 12, but a few exceptional beings go even higher.

There is a reason the number of Gates are reliant on genetics - the mortal body can only withstand the circulation of so many Gates. Going beyond the predetermined number (namely through artificial means) can destroy one's body. 

#### Fracture

Much like trying to lift heavier than one's muscle can withstand, cycling too much Etherion through Gates can cause them to **Fracture**. A fractured Gate is unhealable and can cause lifelong crippling, especially for [[Etharii]]. As such, training and enhancing Gates is of the utmost priority for all who use them. In fact, it is often cited that it is not the quantity of Gates that determines the better but the quality. 

A common way to see how well-trained one's Gates are is through the consistency and flavor of their bodily liquids.

#### Catharsis
^2af867

However, these Gates can eventually be clogged, especially though bodily wear, Etherion overuse, or mental tension. As such, especially among those with high-stress occupations and activities, it is necessary to periodically unclog them through a process known as **Catharsis**.

Catharsis is a form of intimate massage called Agis-Sal where an individual (normally the practitioner) circulates their Etherion through the Gates of the receiver, flushing out any buildup or residue. It involves massaging and sexual pleasuring (in addition to tools like oils and incense), and is considered both pleasurous and relaxing. Many times clients will fall asleep during Catharsis. 

Learning Catharsis requires undergoing rigorous training, which is taught through various school and Exedis Sanctums. These practitioners are called **Agisi**, and are highly prized. Agisi need not be Lynxes to perform their work. In the Greater Realms, there is no concept of Agisi as [[Ais]] are taught Catharsis as children, and have unlimited time to perfect their craft. 

Ordinary folk only need Catharsis done every few years, and even it would take a skilled Agisi only a few minutes to cleanse them. Hard laborers and soldiers need Catharsis done every few months to a year depending on their work. In special cases, like Lynxes or great warriors, Catharsis must be done regularly ranging from every few days to a week. 

Agisi offer their services to specialized parlors known as **Small Shops**, which can be found all over [[Nurauc]] and [[Ascendancy of Duske|Duske]]. How these are treated depends on the culture. Some more prudish areas treat Small Shops as akin to normal brothels and thus obscene (going to them when married is infidelity), while others like Duske treat them as a necessity (there is no prejudice going there married).   ^75ecdc

There are many varieties of Catharsis, unique to different cultures. Some are one on one, while others may be done in groups. 

#### Crucis 

^6b8dcf
In order to maximize their efficacy, Gates need to be continuously worn down and trained. This is important for both Etharii and non-Etharii. The best way to train Gates is through rigorous Catharsis, a process known as **Crucis**.

Crucis acts more like a competition than a cooperation, akin to a sparring match that only ends with one's victory or defeat. During Crucis, practitioners try to subdue each other by flooding their Gates with as much as Etherion as they can, a process that causes intense exhaustion and simulation. It concludes only when the one's body cannot continue any further. It is essentially a brute force strategy that forcibly circulate Gates, so as to gain an increased mastery over their operation and function. Naturally, such methods result in extensive side-effects, including fatigue, hunger, and temporary Gate fracturing. Like muscle training, these effects decrease through practice, and serve to make practitioners stronger. 


The practice of Crucis has died down over the centuries as it became considered primitive, violent, and obscene, the complete opposite of the elegant and refined Catharsis. A number of alternative methods have come into light over the recent few centuries, including **Resonance** - a less effective method that involves having a partner press one's Gate and channeling Etherion through it. It is far less exhaustive and more akin to rehab exercise, but it has slowly come to dominate Gate training due to its simplicity, ease of use, and gentleness. Not to say it cannot be hard, but easy in comparison to Crucis.
