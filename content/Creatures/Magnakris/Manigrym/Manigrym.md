The Manigrym or Moon-Eaters are a race of powerful [[Magnakris]], who dwell secluded within the [[Taryld Duske]]. They can be likened to werewolves, and possess the ability to take the form of species' they mate with.

## Appearance 

The Manigrym are tall and robust. They resemble andromorphic wolves, only with a decidedly more deadly and ferocious appearance. They can stand either bipedal or quadrupedal with no issue. 

Their fur color varies from gray to black; however, silver also sometimes appears, and is considered a symbol of divinity. 
![[Manigrym (Male).png|right lp| 300]]

The Manigrym men are quite tall, their heights averaging around 6'3 to 7'1. They are covered in fur, especially around their limbs and back. Almost all of them are heavily muscled, owing to their hunting culture. 

The women stand slightly shorter, around 5'10 to 6'5. Like the men, their fur is concentrated around their limbs and back, leaving their breasts and torso covered in shorter fur. They are also quite muscular, if not to the size of the men.
