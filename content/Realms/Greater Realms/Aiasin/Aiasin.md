Aiasin is a term for the familia, centered around a [[Gods|God]] and their progeny, the [[Ais]], in the [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms]]. Most have developed into civilizations, with their God erected as a figure of worship. They shelter their Gods in great temples and churches during its slumber, and obey its every whim. 

Every Aiasin differs greatly in culture, language, etc. Many have become grand civilizations over the course of their immensely long history, much longer than any Lower Realm nation. 

The God's instability and aggression to one another filtered down to their Aiasin, leading to most becoming hostile to one another. Wars are common, but it is commonly fought on neutral area rather than either parties' territory. After all, no Ais is idiotic enough to risk awakening a God. 

Furthermore, the volatile nature of the God's, whose wrath could doom both parties, necessitated a very strict and regulated war culture, with many laws and rules over what could and couldn't be done. Eventually, to minimize causalities while still being able to war, their wars turned into duels. That is, a fight between two representatives to decide the winner of the war. 

All Aiasin have a hierarchy based on their degree of relativity to their Divine Father. As such, it is commonly [[Demigod|Demigods]] that head an Aiasin, as they are the direct children of the Gods. 

***

Within an Aiasin, more often than not, incest is common. As breeding with Spawn of different of Gods is frowned upon, Ais normally inbreed with their own family, which normalized incest. And due to being immune to disease or genetic failure, there were no repercussions. Over tens of thousands of years, their incest became normalized, though there was no longer any need for it.
