Lunthiel was born to Audaunis Daymion and his cousin Audaunis Lariel, the latter who died during childbirth. However, by far the most pressing concern was that she was born with the characteristics of a Beast: four arms. It was no mutation, as the four arms each had full range of motion and fit proportionally with the rest of her physique. They were covered in dark fur and clawed, but what was most pressing was the runic patterning of gold that covered her arms and legs. It appeared akin to a language, which only served to reinforce Lunthiel's divinity. 

A Beast is not something to be taken lightly. Even by [[Timeline#^e9b1ac|Dominus]], Zenelith does not have the resources or firepower to subdue a Beast. While thankfully most are sealed or sleeping, and Beast are no more than a wayward legend, that doesn't mean Lunthiel could be let of lightly. Even the merest whisper of the fact that Duske is holding, no, *raising* a Beast is enough for other nations to collectively come together to wipe it out. Yet Lunthiel was not truly a Beast, at least not yet. While possessing the mark of the [[Many-Fanged One]], the four arms, she has yet to display any power. And indeed, there has never been anything saying a Beast could be born to panhumans, or indeed even look human. Still, the fact remained that Lunthiel was a threat to all of mankind, and must be killed. 

The day of her birth was a secretive affair, though it was said that upon giving birth Lariel died looking as though her very soul was devoured. Upon taking out the baby, the midwife herself screamed and ran, though she was caught soon after. Daymion had her killed her, alongside whoever knew of her birth to keep the secret from leaking out. Afterwards, he took Lunthiel, bringing her too the Duske Spire to consult with his wife. Daymion had many enemies during his tenure, and it would be unwise to consult any without first asking Zynaria for help. Zynaria then suggested he consult the heads of the Kynodus, who would doubtless be pleased at the birth of Lunthiel - most of the Kynodus worshipped their ancestor, the Beast of Kyn, and they would take Lunthiel to be the Beast reincarnated. Zynaria herself was convinced in slaying the babe, though she was reluctantly reasoned out of it: first was that Beasts were considered the children of the [[Many-Fanged One]]. While the Gods normally wouldn't notice the death of their child, the Beast God wasn't like other Gods, and there was no telling whether he would retaliate or not. It was safer to keep Lunthiel alive than risk all of Zenelith. Second, Duske itself was torn by the vying [[Vael|Vaels]], each of whom desired the throne. They needed something that would cow them into submission, though the issue remained of hiding Lunthiel's identity. And, perhaps most importantly, Duske needed a weapon, a weapon strong enough to cow the powerful, ancient civilizations that ruled Zenelith. And Lunthiel was, or rather could be, the key.

Lunthiel spent her early years in the [[Duske Spire|Spire]], where she was taught by Zynaria along with a few selected tutors. She was covered in long, loose robes that hid her extra arms, hiding her identity. Eventually, when Lunthiel was around six, she grew difficult to manage due to her absurd physical strength and active nature. It was becoming too risky to remain in the Spire, and Zynaria decided it would be best to send her off somewhere where she can continue to grow.

She gathered trustworthy and competent personnel, ranging from servants to [[Combatsmith|combatsmiths]], warriors, and Lynxes. Among them included the best of the Kynodus warriors. 

They were to raise and train Lunthiel, in a remote facility in the corner of Duske where Lunthiel would spend her childhood. Zynaria also chose her nine year old daughter [[Audaunis Arin]], Lunthiel's half-sister to come with her. If what Daymion believed came to pass, the two would be need to have a close bond to ensure Duske's prosperity. Arin would be trained and tutored as well, under the guise of studying abroad.

Lunthiel, Arin, and their aides (who would form the initial [[Haven]]) trained for the next ten years. While both performed well beyond what was expected, Lunthiel outstripped Arin tenfold in the arts of war, though Arin performed better in other subjects. After around five years, Arin left to attend the [[Royal Academy of Laitskaer]], leaving Lunthiel with the aides by herself. She stayed with them for another four years, before Arin returned from the Academy to assume the throne upon her father's death. Lunthiel at this point was far and away stronger than her instructors, and was ready to take on the role of Ascendant. She was given [[Dawnchain#^c3016e|Sunshard]], the armor worn by [[Kynod the Sun]], to mark her status as Ascendant. The armor reacted to Lunthiel, and she forged [[Dawnchain]] with it.

Lunthiel returned to the [[Duske Spire]], given the [[Inner Spire]] as her quarters. She stayed mostly within there, unable to leave without her guardian Arin's explicit permission. She was only granted leave on specific outings, mostly to cow a [[Lorde]] or [[Vael]] into submission, all the while disguised. At the time, research was being done on Lunthiel's blood. Such a thing would be considered heresy, but Duske was not one to give a thought to such things. It wasn't until the arrival of Apocanyser that the blood would find its use: creating the [[Executioners]].
