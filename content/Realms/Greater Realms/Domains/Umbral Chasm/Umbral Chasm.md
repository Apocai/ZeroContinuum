The Umbral Chasm is the domain of the [[Helldragon]], situate in the [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms]]. 

The Umbral Chasm is often described in various mythologies across the [[Lesser Realms]] as Hell, and the term is indeed apt.

The Chasm is a forbidden place, even within the Greater Realms. Many eons ago, during the [[Timeline#^faf709|Age of Shadow]], it was a battleground for a nameless war between the [[Gods]] and invading [[Great Hermits]]. The defeated Gods and Great Hermits became static, their bodies making up the islands of the Chasm. 

## Appearance:

The Chasm is an empty void holding massive, floating islands, beneath which lies a giant, black sun. Before the war, the void was an endless blue sky, its islands lush and teeming with life. However, the corruption left by the divine corpses has warped the place, changing its sky to an endless abyss, and its islands to reflect the twisted nature of the Chasm. 

The islands vary in size, shape, and appearance. Some are filled in fire and brimstone with rivers of molten ichor, while others are plains of violet flowers, a seemingly innocent thing. They are connected to one another by long, massive chains, known as "bridges". These bridges are the only means of traversal from one island to another, and are guarded by [[Gargoyles|gargoyles]].

The islands are not all of the same level, many being higher or lower than others. The lowest is not an island, but rather a massive palace-like structure: the [[White Citadel]], residence of [[Daemonium Theana]] the First [[Demon Kings]] and is perched directly above the black sun, the slumbering form of the Helldragon. 

This black sun is what lights the Chasm, bathing it in a peaceful, violet hue. The sun gives off no heat; instead, the islands themselves emit heat, as though they were living beings...

## Inhabitants:
 
There are inhabitants, known as [[Demon|demons]], that live within the Umbral Chasm. These beings are the remnants of the deceased Gods' [[Aiasin]], renegade [[Ais]] who have defied their God and/or Aiasin, and wanderers, inn addition to [[Akashraal|Akashraal]] and other creatures. 

All within the Chasm become corrupted by the Helldragon's presence, changing their bodies and granting them power. The effects differ among the inhabitants, but most grow features like horns, tails, etc. that make qualifiable as demons, despite sharing different origins. They are granted great power by this transformation, but in exchange gradually have the skills born by their genetic heritage removed. The power they are granted vary, and are dependent on the being themselves. 

## Ruling:

The Sanctum is split into holds, and is ruled by the [[Helldragon's Court]]. The Court consists of the  [[Demon Kings]], who each rule a separate hold. There is no concept of tax or government within the Sanctum; instead the Demon Kings manage the affairs of their people and ensure they do not fall into chaos. The Demon Kings have the title "Daemonium", of which Theana is the greatest.
