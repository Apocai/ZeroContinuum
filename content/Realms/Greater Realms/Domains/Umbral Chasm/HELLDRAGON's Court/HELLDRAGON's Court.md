The Helldragon's Court is made up of the 6 [[Demon Kings|Demon Kings]] of the [[Umbral Chasm]] and is ruled by [[Avarasin]], the [[Gods#^6ece88|Incarnation]] of the [[Helldragon]].  

As Avarasin is usually absent, either because she is out exploring or has been assimilated into the Helldragon, the de-facto leader is the First Demon King, [[Daemonium Theana]]. 

They are:

I -  Theana, Demon of the Abyss
II -  [[Mazain]] the Flayer
III -  [[Ranzathara]] the Frenzied
IV - [[Arcanthor]] the Serene
V - [[Kara]] the Berserker
VI - [[Yuwerti]] the Silver

## Origin:

The first Demon Kings were chosen by Theana personally following the [[Thousand-Fold Conquest]]. She offered the greatest of the warriors, those whose [[Gods]] had been defeated and had their Aiasins annihilated, a chance to serve under her lord. All accepted and were assigned territories to rule how they chose fit. 

The title of Demon King is not hereditary, and is instead chosen by strength- the successor of the Demon King is the one who kills them in combat. All of the original Demon Kings had eventually been defeated, and as new ones took up the title, they too were eventually defeated. Some were defeated more than others, but all that became Demon King were incredibly powerful.
