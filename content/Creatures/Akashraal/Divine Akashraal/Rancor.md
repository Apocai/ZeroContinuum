Rancor is a [[Akashraal#^7a1890|Divine Akashraal]]  worshiped by the [[Manigrym]], having watched over their clan since antiquity. It is not a [[Beast|Beast]] like the [[Beast of Omen]] or [[Beast of Kyn]]. 

![[Rancor.png| right lp| 500]]

#### Appearance:

Rancor is a large, vaguely tiger-like creature, its greatest characteristics being the great wings upon its back. 

Its shoulder height is around 8.5 feet tall and is incredibly muscular, with powerful hindlegs and strong forelimbs. 

It has white fur coating its body, with golden stone-like plating and feathers

#### Abilities

Rancor has immense strength and power, as can be seen by his physique. On top of that, he is surprisingly fast for his size, little more than a blur in the eyes of the common man. 

As an Akashraal, he is resistant to most physical attacks, and even then has passive [[Etherion]]
