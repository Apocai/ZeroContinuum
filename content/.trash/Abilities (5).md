##### Divine Intellect

Nerathys was built with an extraordinary mind, one capable of thought and computation akin to a supercomputer. It can simulate outcomes, multitask different operations simultaneously, and aid greatly in the speed of casting [[Cipher|Ciphers]].

##### Clairvoyance of Mind

By using her Divine Intellect, Nerathys can simulate possibilities in the near future, effectively allowing her to predict the future. Such a feat is night omnipotent against most foes, though it can be countered, namely if they are strong enough to where it doesn't matter, or possess abilities that can negate Clairvoyance. 

##### Clairvoyance of Body

As she was made for the slaying of ORDER, the [[Runis Modrath]] ensured that is the only course available to her. That is, she cannot cease until such a thing has transpired. 

True immortality was impossible in the face of such a threat, so there needed to be a more surefire way of retaining her existence. And so they gave her the culmination of their heretical intellect: Clairvoyance of Body. This ability is essentially time looping. It will seem like reality  will "edit" itself to avoid her death. Nerathys will die, and be forced to return to a specific point, called a save. The saves are randomly created, and can range from a few seconds to years. However, in [[Creation]], there is only one timeline, the Zero Continuum. All other timelines are pruned by ORDER, leaving only one sequence of events. Therefore, to avoid becoming erased, Nerathys must ensure she reaches nearly the same order of events she had before she died. Such a thing is calculated by [[DOMINION]] upon every death, and allows Nerathys to understand the consequences of every action she takes. 

Take note that it is *nearly* the same order, not the same order. There is a slight margin of error she can abuse to ensure the odds are turned to her favor. It is only due to such a reason that Nerathys can overcome a seemingly inescapable death. Furthermore, the power she has culminated prior to her death is retained, letting her get stronger with every loop.
