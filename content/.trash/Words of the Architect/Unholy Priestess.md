The Unholy Priestess, also known as the Eldritch Slut, is the scribe of the [[Dark Architect]], who records Its words upon her throne in the[[ Silent Library]]. She is an otherworldly being, who can find pleasure only in the consumption and understanding of knowledge and information, regardless of its content. 

Her throne, on its seat, has jutting out of it the **Quill**, a long cylindrical rod that is inserted into the anus when the Eldritch Slut rests. It spews **Ink** into her bowels, which is then used by her tentacles to inscribe the words of the Architect onto her body.
