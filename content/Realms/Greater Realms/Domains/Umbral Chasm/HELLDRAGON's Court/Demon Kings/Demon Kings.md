Demon Kings, numbering six, are the lords of the [[Umbral Chasm]], each governing a [[Holds]] granted by the First King [[Daemonium Theana]]. They form the [[Helldragon's Court]], which is headed by [[Avarasin]], the [[Helldragon|Helldragon's]] Incarnation. 


## Power:

The Demon Kings are all, without a doubt, powerful. They are the strongest entities in the Umbral Sanctum.
