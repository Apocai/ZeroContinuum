Bloodchain is a [[Chain|Primechain]] granted through Chaining with [[The Bleeding King]]. Unlike other Chains, what's special about this one is that the Bleeding One is static, which begs the question of how exactly one creates a Chain with it. 

As of now, only one user of Bloodchain exists: [[Nerathys]]. She had created a Chain with its very body in [[Kuzaria]], where it lies at the very bottom of the underwater [[Anvil Crater]], the site where the [[Kuzarian Hammerblow]] struck. 

### Abilities:

Bloodchain, as the name suggests, primarily relies on blood to operate. It requires the user regularly consume panhuman blood, as doing so is both one of the edicts of the Chain and a requirement to use its abilities. There is also a lurking hunger for blood, and there is no satiation for the hunger, no matter how blood one consumes. 

The hunger for blood replaces all other urges. For Nerathy's case, as she does not require food or water, only Etherion to power her, the blood replaces her need for Etherion.

If blood is not consumed for a period of time, the user will weaken, until eventually dying.

The following abilities pertain mostly to Nerathys, as she is the sole user.


##### Reconstruct (Blood-Variance):

By drinking a Lynx's blood, Nerathys can replicate their Chain permanently. In addition, any [[Cipher|Ciphers]] she uses will be enhanced, becoming a **Blood-Variant**. 

A Blood-Variant changes the Cipher's property to blood, while improving the [[Tempering]] of the Cipher by one. 

In exchange for such a boost in power, using a Blood-Variant replaces the Etherion cost with blood, draining her blood supply. Furthermore, only four Chains may be reconstructed at a time, as going over capacity will result in a weakening of all acquired Chains. Of course, any Blood Variants she acquires need to be properly learned, same as for any other Lynx. It is only due to Nerathys's great intellect that she can use them within a few minutes of reconstructing a Chain. 

##### Legion (Domination of Death Through Blood)

By slaying a foe, and feeding the corpse Nerathys's blood, Nerathys can transform them into a powerful type of [[Undead|undead]] known as a Crimson [[Echofiend]]. These Echofiends retain (and sometimes even improve) the abilities and skills they had while alive. They cannot speak, but possess some level of intelligence. 

They can be summoned by Nerathys at will, and obey any directive, no matter how mundane. Their greatest strength lies in being able to drink and send blood to Nerathys, the caveat lying in that this does not replace her  consuming blood as sustenance, nor does it alleviate her thirst.  

The Crimson Echofiends are absorbed into Nerathys's soul, allowing them to travel with her into the past. As they exist within her soul, they can easily be summoned or dismissed at will, no matter the time or location. 

The core of this ability is that Nerathys does not strictly transform the corpses: rather, she copies their body and soul, effectively creating another version of them. As both their souls and bodies are artifcial, they are considered more akin to machines than living beings, and are thus removed from the [[Gwinvarga]], making them immortal. If destroyed, they are sent back into Nerathys, where they will take time to heal. 

Their appearance is warped from when they were alive, with red skin, hair, etc. They look demonic in appearance.
