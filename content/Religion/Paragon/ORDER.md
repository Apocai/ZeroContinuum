ORDER is the one of the embodiments of the Universe, and its current ruler. It is without equal, a being beyond all else.

It is one of two oldest beings (alongside its counterpart [[CHAOS]]) in the Universe, having existed since before time itself. More in [[Continuum Infinus]].

Order serves to maintain equilibrium and balance in the Universe, as such regarding all things impassively and equally. It desires not the complete dominance and submission of all beings, instead becoming the enforcer of [[LAW]] to maintain balance within [[Creation]]. And those that break it will be subject to its punishment.

However, it has broken from the Continuum Infinus, leading to yet another iteration of [[Creation]], which is known thus as the [[Zero Continuum]]. 

It prunes timelines that do not align with its unknown objective, and head Creation to a future unlike any seen before.
