The Bleeding King is the [[Gods|God]] of what became humanity, and is static. It became so during the end of the [[Great Sundering]], where it was struck by a great attack named the [[Kuzarian Hammerblow]] by the [[Helldragon]].

Its identity is shrouded in mystery, save for its [[Chain]]: [[Bloodchain]], which continues to exist even after the God became static. 

Perhaps most intriguing is that it created the first [[Ais]] and thus the first [[Aiasin]]. These Ais, upon the Bleeding One's defeat, wondered across [[Zenelith]], becoming modern day [[Human|humans]].
