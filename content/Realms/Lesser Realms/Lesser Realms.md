The Lesser Realms are a broad term for the physical side of reality, the pure scientific aspect of the [[Creation]]. It is consists of outer space, as well as the many planets, stars, and other celestial objects that are found in it.

It is home to many beings, including the [[Logi]] (an umbrella term for sapient beings), though of vastly different races and cultures between planets. There is almost no intermingling between sapients of different planets due to the [[UNKNOWN]], who space-travel to dangerous. The creation of [[Zenelith]]'s [[Fluxgate]] have mitigated this somewhat, though presently the technology is under their control.

There is only one group of creatures able to freely traverse outer space: The [[Abominations]]. They alone are the exception, not through cunning or cleverness, but through sheer force of might.
