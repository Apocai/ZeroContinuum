Panhumanity are an umbrella term for the humanoid races of [[Creation]]. The name comes from human, as humans (who are included under panhumanity) are the -lone sapient race, that has appeared in every [[Continuum Infinus|iteration]] of Creation, though their fates varied-. As such, many other panhumans are related to them, through artificial and biological means.  

Panhumans typically have long lifespans (discounting humans), and age slower as they grow older.
