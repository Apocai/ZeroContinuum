
Nerathys was created to be the perfect being, and her form reflects that. Her appearance is, for lack of a better word, perfect, though with such perfection comes a certain... unnaturalness to her features. One cannot shake the fact that there is something distinctly sinister about her, though the feeling fades as they become enamored with her beauty. 

Her face is almost doll-like, with clear porcelain-like skin that seem to almost glow. Such features would look best without expression, yet Nerathys tends to twists them into blood-curdling smiles and grins. Snow-white hair flows freely from her head, reaching almost her ankles. It is worn in braids and ties, though they seem to inevitably untangle within the heat of combat. 

Her body is a masterwork of craftsmanship, forged to shadow even the most beautiful of sculptures. Her form is perfectly proportionate, well-muscled yet not overburdened by it. Her graceful physique is complemented by the swells of her femininity, yet there is something almost predatorial in such beauty.

#### Clothing

Nerathys's clothing consists only of the [[Omenchain#^79706a|Fell-Graft]], as it can not be taken off. The Fell-Graft is made up of the innerwear and outer armor. The innerwear's appearance can be transformed, though the transformations can only decrease material from the default. 

###### Innerwear:

The innerwear consists only of a skin-tight, onyx black leotard. The back is open, exposing Nerathys's exospine completely. Needless to say, an outfit such as this is wholly unsuited for daily life. Its extremely revealing nature can only assure that the wearer is undoubtedly an exhibitionist. 

Perhaps the most noticeable feature is the quality of the fabric itself. It is almost flowing, an unsettling dark like the abyss itself. And every now and then, it seems almost like the red eyes of a beast glimmer in its midst.
