The UNKNOWN are beings of unknown origin, appearance, and size, under the domain of [[CHAOS]]. They prowl throughout the Lesser Realms, devouring information, data, and basically anything that stands with ORDER, all in a vain attempt to satiate a hunger that can only be relieved by consuming the [[Gods]]. Their very existence spreads madness and corruption, and have made the space incredibly dangerous and a literal death sentence. However, for all their power, they stay away from celestial bodies with a [[World Factor]], as such things are the Bastion of [[ORDER]].

They can corrupt information and data, which is why their existence is a secret known to very few across [[Creation]]. 

An example of this is from [[Sol-3]], also known as Earth, where the inhabiting humans have launched many space probes, telescopes, rockets, and shuttles.

The truth is that all the information Earth has received is twisted by the [[UNKNOWN]], to avoid information about them becoming widespread. Images of the wider Creation is faked to only show what Earth wants to see, with none of the unholy monstrosities that hunt throughout it. Not only that, but Earth's humanity has created space stations on the Moon and Mars, which they believe they are regularly in contact with. Of course, as the Moon does not possess a [[World Factor]] and Mars is a dead planet, the UKNOWN have already devoured all humans there, but are continuing to send false information back. Inexplicably, despite there being no one alive on the bases  researchers, scientists, and astronauts do go back to visit routinely, though they are described by people around them as acting strange or unlike themselves.  

Such is the case for many civilizations that are unaware of the dangers of space, and readily push ahead with astronomical activities. The UKNOWN prevent their existence from becoming known, so that people will continue to be sent out into space and get devoured. This has led to the eventual doom of many civilizations across Creation, though very few know about it. This is somewhat reminiscent of the Dark Forest Theory, as the UKNOWN become aware of a civilization first though their sent signals, data, probes, etc.  

**Origin**

They are the larva of what will become the Gods of [[Lost Paradise]], who have escaped from the end of time and blindly hunt for the Gods who would pave the way to their ascension.-
