Omenchain is forged through the [[Chain#^da9a1f|Chaining]] between a [[Lynx]] and the sentient armor known as the **Fell-Graft**.

Omenchain possesses [[Curse|Curses]] instead of the traditional [[Cipher]], which are imbued directly into the Fell-Graft, and can only be used when wearing the armor. 

The present wielder of the Fell-Graft is [[Nerathys]].

***
The Fell-Graft is a sentient armor created from the corpse of the [[Fellhound]], a great [[Akashraal]] that once terrorized the [[Ever-Luminous Holy Imperium of Solestine]]. The fact that the Fellhound's body didn't disintegrate like other Akashraal is due to the Omenchain, which preserved the body even after death.  ^79706a

Even after having been forged into an armor, the Fellhound's influence still corrupts the wearer's mind, making them more bloodthirsty and bestial.

### Abilities:

The Fell-Graft cannot be taken off once, though it can be transformed into a few outfits. It is made up of two layers: the innerwear and the plate.  ^b8be2c

##### Curse of the Hunt

The wearer's physical abilities surge tremendously, and they can fight comfortably on all fours. In addition, it is highly resistant to Etherion, making it the natural enemy of [[Lynx|Lynxes]]. Furthermore, the armor also comes with a razor sharp, prehensile tail, that can be used as naturally as one's arm. 

However, it passively arouses the wearer, making them unable to fully concentrate during combat. Such a thing rises from the Fellhound's lustful nature, where it would once take Logoi females as slaves, to be raped and toyed with as it pleased. 

##### Curse of Gluttony

The wearer must regularly consume fresh flesh and blood to maintain their strength. Many of the Fell-Graft's abilities will be disabled if going on to long without eating. While any living being will though, only panhumans can truly satisfy them. 
##### Curse of the Mighty

Nerathys's presence will strike terror into those who deem themselves lesser than her. It cannot be turned off, though the effect is dampened when only wearing the innerwear

##### Curse of the Omenshard

The armor can manifest the **Omenshard**, a long, nigh-indestructible, black odachi. It has an innate advantage against living organisms and that which cannot be cut. It's main drawback is that it can only cut those things, and nothing else. It would fail to even cut paper.
