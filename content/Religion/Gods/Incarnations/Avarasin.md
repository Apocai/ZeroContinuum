Avarasin is the [[Gods#^6ece88|Incarnation]] of the [[Helldragon]], and ruler of the [[Helldragon's Court]]. She is responsible for taking in [[Apocanyser]] and [[Nerathys]], and they refer to her as their Divine Sister (believing she is too lacking in maturity to be seen as anything beyond an older sister). 

#### Overview

Avarasin is a free-spirited woman who, when manifested, spends her time exploring the [[Lesser Realms]]. She is a curious and decidedly child-like individual who is eager to learn new things and speak her mind. She is also rather chunni, meaning that she has a strange appreciation for things that are "cool" to her. This can include flashy outfits, names, and other things. 

Of course, that isn't to say she is innocent either. She believes strongly in a hierarchical difference between the weak and strong, though what she classifies as either is beyond common sense. 

She is not easily fazed, or rather, not fazed at all. No matter what she witnesses or experiences, she approaches it with an innocent excitement. Nor does she experience anger or sadness, as a being of her might is above such things. However, lust is something she experiences, though it is mostly targeted towards other women (chief among them being [[Daemonium Theana]]). 

Still, she is extremely perceptive of others, and possesses great wisdom, as is befitting of a God. 

##### Physiology and Appearance

As an Incarnation, Avarasin's body is but a construction of [[Etherion]], and is only shaped in the image of a living organism. She possesses no bodily functions or organs, though she can create them easily if necessary. 
![[Avarasin.png| right lp| 500]]
Her physical appearance itself is beautiful. She stands at around 6'5, with a willowy and voluptuous physique. Hard red scales lines her body, and her limbs are decidedly draconic. Her inner body is has pale red skin, with faint markings across them. 

She possesses great demonic horns jutting from her head, as well thick, flowing crimson hair that reaches to her legs. Her eyes are like twin suns, burning with a divine flame. Most stunning of all are the great wings on her back, easily dwarfing her in size. While she does not need them to fly, she nevertheless added it because it "looked cool".

She tends not to wear clothing, baring her body to the world. If needed however, she can envelop her delicates in scales.
