## **Overview**

The Warden is the name of the artificial soul harbored within Apocanyser. She is an ego of [[Nerathys]], a failsafe she created to reign in Apocanyser. However, while Nerathys and the Warden are the same person, Warden represents a more innocent, pure Nerathys before she became corrupted by her loops. She also functions as [[Arsenal]], the central command system of [[Haven]].

## **Appearance**

The Warden's appearance reflected a fantasy held by Nerathys during her early loops, one where she was related by blood to Apocanyser and Serana. As such, the Warden's appearance is based off what Nerathys would have looked like if she was Apocanyser's blood sister (at the time she was curious about Apocanyser's origins). 

The Warden appears to be in her early to mid teens. While most of Nerathys's features still remain, her skin is dusky and her eyes the same steelish cast of Apocanyser. Her hair did not change too much, though it went from ghostly-white to a more ashen-grey. It hangs to her mid-back, and has a more wavy than straight texture. 

Her body is slim and lean, yet to possess the powerful, beautiful form Nerathys holds. Yet traces of what is to come can be seen in the Warden's shape, from the budding bosom and curving hips to the long limbs and straight back.

## **Abilities**

The Warden's main functionality lies in data processing, information gathering, and communication. It has no offensive ability, though it can take control of many of the Arsenal's weapons. (See [[Arsenal]]).

#### Apocanyser

The Warden serves as Apocanyser's soul, and gives him access to many useful features.

She can  display to Apocanyser's eyes something similar to an in-game HUD overlay, which can show many times including markers, analytics, etc. She can also connect to Apocanyser's [[Gazer]].

However, her greatest ability is Oversoul,  where she tries to push her soul physically out of Apocanyser's body. As such a thing would go against the tenants of [[ORDER]], it enables Apocai's body to strengthen itself.

To be able to all this, she makes use of Apocanyser's Gates, as he himself is unable to do so.
