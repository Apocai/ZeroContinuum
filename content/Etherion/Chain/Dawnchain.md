Dawnchain is received through wearing the Sunshard, an armor forged out of the body of the Kynodus's divine progenitor: the Beast of Kyn. It has never been used since its creation, having lain dormant and unresponsive to any that tried to claim it. That is, till [[Lunthiel]].   

The primary function of Dawnchain is the siphoning of energy. Its abilities rely on collecting and storing energy to be used for the Lynx's purpose. 

**Plunder-** The wearer can absorb an object's kinetic energy when in close range, taking active concentration and awareness. 

**Lightbreaker**- When using the stored energy, the wearer can accelerate herself to ludicrous levels. They cannot use Plunder at the same time. Their body is protected from the acceleration by the armor. While the wearer's perception and reaction time are increased by Sunshard, it is not enough to fully process the environment, and as such should not be used in enclosed spaces without the appropriate skill and caution. 

**Oppression**- A passive domain that extends around 300 feet in every direction. Energy is drained from all things within the domain, at a slow, yet fixed rate. By pinpointing the energy drain, the wearer can have a general visual of the domain's layout, including the structure and location of living things. The draining can increase in strength, but that requires substantial concentration and effort.

Unlike the [[Omenchain#^79706a|Fell-Graft]], the will of the Kynbeast does not affect the wearer. Additionally, the armor can be summoned and dispelled without consequence, and the wearer does not need to wear the armor to use Dawnchain.

Sunshard provides an overall boost in physical and cognitive ability, as well as maintains a continuous, small version of Plunder around the wearer's body. But perhaps its most useful feature is **Heavenbreaker**, named so due to its complimenting of Lightbreaker. Heavenbreaker transforms the wearer into light and allows them to move at the speed of light, though the energy cost is massive and thus cannot be used for more than a second or two. Travel is normally limited to straight lines, due to the cognitive limitations in being forced to redirect at such speeds.

Due to the excessive costs with Heavenbreaker, it is normally used only for the smallest frames of time the wearer can activate and deactivate it- in the span of milliseconds.
