A gazer is a small Etherion device that is placed upon one's right temple, and is used much like a phone. 

It's most stunning feature is the ability to project an almost seamless projection in front of the user's eyes, which can be activated on deactivated easily. Its contents are visible only to the user, and the display can be shrunk or expanded. The gazer also comes with the ability to call and speak.

There are many different models made by different corporations. They specialize in different things, including civilian use, military use, etc.
