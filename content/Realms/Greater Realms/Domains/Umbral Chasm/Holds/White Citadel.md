The White Citadel is near the bottom of the [[Umbral Chasm]], and is perched right above the Sanctum's black sun, the slumbering form of the [[Helldragon]]. It is one of six [[Holds]] in the Chasm, and is ruled over by [[Daemonium Theana]].

There is no island holding the Citadel. It floats above the black sun, connected by ivory chains that attach to a ring encircling the structure. The ring has bridges that connect it to other islands far above. 

The Citadel itself is vaguely oblong in shape, and extends down until it makes contact with the sun. It is made of an unknown, ivory material and is immense in size, holding numerous rooms and hallways. Many of the areas are uninhabited.

It's architecture resembles that of a Gothic Cathedral, but with a noticeably alien twist. The walls themselves are luminescent, eliminating any need for external light sources.

## Inhabitants:

The only inhabitants are its master Theana, her students the [[Acolytes]], the various servants, and at times [[Serana]], with both [[Nerathys]] and [[Apocanyser]] having left long ago, one by choice and another through accident.
