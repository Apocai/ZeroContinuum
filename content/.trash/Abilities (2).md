## **Abilities**

##### Combat Ability:

Arin is an extremely formidable warrior, owing to both her natural talent and hard work. She is skilled in hand-to-hand combat, and is particularly skilled in the bow and spear. 

##### Chain

Arin, despite doing well in the [[Royal Academy of Laitskaer]] and being an [[Etharii]], does not have much aptitude as a [[Lynx]]. She has forged [[Wardchain]], but has used her abilities to train her [[Gate|Gates]] rather than progress as a Lynx. 

Through union with [[Khuldran]], Arin forged [[Moonchain]]. Normally, Lynxes cannot manage two Chains: owing to the unique nature of Chains relating to the [[Many-Fanged One]], Moonchain devoured Wardchain. 

##### Gates

Arin possesses 4 Gates, all of which are properly trained. 

She has maintained her maintenance and training since her days at the academy. However, after becoming the Night Priestess of the the Manigrym she also started regularly training through [[Gate#^6b8dcf|Crucis]], which helped greatly in improving her Gates. 

##### Titan

As the Titan, Arin is skilled in diplomacy, negotiation, clerical duties, among others. That being said, her short-temper and inability to bend before the will of others has at times caused issues. Of course, Arin being Arin, all issues were resolved.
