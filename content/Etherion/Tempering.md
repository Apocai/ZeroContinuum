
Tempering is the name given to the level of attunement one has to their [[Chain]], equivalent to how close a [[Lynx]] is to their [[Chain#^9b2de5|Master]]. The greater the attunement, the better and stronger [[Cipher|Ciphers]] they can, though it comes at a price - the price of their humanity. 

As a Lynx's Tempering grows, they understand more intrinsically the inner workings of the Chain, and become closer to divinity.
