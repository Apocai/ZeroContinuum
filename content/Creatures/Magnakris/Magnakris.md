 Magnakris are a powerful species of [[Akashraal]]. As their name suggests, they are considered by many to be ancient relatives of the [[Onikris]]. They are among the most intelligent species of Akashraal, having their own language, culture, and society.

They are few in number, and vary greatly in appearance and characteristics, just like the Onikris. However, they have far greater physical capabilities, as well as [[Etherion]] affinity. 

#### Appearance

They resemble Onikris, though they are much larger and bestial. They can alternate between bipedal and quadrupedal stances, and have no preference. 

#### Classification

They are classified as Akashraal despite their humanoid appearances due to bias regarding their stronger relation to the [[Many-Fanged One]] (whom many actively worship), lack of desire to participate in panhuman society, and physical appearance (when on all fours they resemble Akashraal far more than humans).

In addition, other factors include their biological similarity to Akashraal, such that they can reproduce with them (and do so regularly.
