Bright is a material derived from [[Ortherium]]. It can exist in liquid and solid states: liquid is used primarily as an exceptional fuel source while solid is used as a material. 

#### Process

To create liquid Bright, Ortherium must first be melted using **Brightfire**. Brightfire, as the name suggests, comes from burning liquid Bright. Before Brightfire could be used, the first Bright was created through the fiery breath of a [[Dragon|dragon]], which held similar properties. Brightfire burns for a very long time untended (years), and is extremely hot. It is also very clean, dispensing not ash but rather a pink, luminescent exhaust. 

Afterwards, the melted Ortherium must then be shone on by concentrated light from the [[Ral-Varsha]], using a set of specialized lens. After a few hours, the molten-red ore will turn to pinkish crimson, signifying the creation of Bright. 

The process to create a special form of solid Bright, known as **Brightsteel**, and forge using it is far more difficult. Liquid Bright must be left to cool in the Ral-Varsha's lunar light for a few days, until it gains a solid shape. Then, it must be doused in liquid Bright, and worked on while it is burning in Brightfire. The metal must be worked on continuously, wreathed in the fire the whole while. There can be no breaks, and the only cooling done is by the lunar light. As the finishing touch, Brightsteel must be coated in blood. The underlying reason is that the blood reacts with the Brightsteel, reinforcing the metal and ensuring it will not wear or dull. 

#### Usage:

Bright has been used widely in civilizations across [[Zenelith]] during the [[Timeline#^207884|Kindling]], though its use as a fuel source declined with the advent of [[Etherion]]. However, due to Etherion's volatile nature and inability to be used in regions with unstable Etherion fluctuations, Bright still remains in high use, especially for weaponry. 

**Liquid Bright** is used as a fuel source. While discovered much earlier, the process of actually converting Ortherium to Bright was only found out during Brightus. After this, Bright was used as as a light source, before being used for combustion. 

A few centuries after that, Bright began to be used as a direct energy source - much like a battery. Liquid Bright, when experiencing high temperature fluctuations, becomes extremely unstable, able to combust with the slightest difference in pressure. This knowledge helped the development of many new inventions, which at the time progressed panhumanity to a new era. Now days, in [[Timeline#^e9b1ac|Dominus]], Liquid Bright is used primarily in firing weapons, as well for technology in regions where Etherion tech is rendered unusable (i.e. areas with high Etherion instability) alongside electricity and nuclear power.  

On the other hand, **Solid Bright** is a natural conduit for Etherion, able to safely attune and channel it. As such, it is used heavily when building Etherion machinery and infrastructure, though, due to the scarcity of the material, it is only regulated to the most most important ones. Other, lesser metals are used in most cases, though they have no where near as much conductivity.


#### Sunsmiths and Sunforges

The forges used to smith such Brightsteel are called **Sunforges**, while the smiths who forge them are called **Sunsmiths**. These men are highly acclaimed throughout Zenelith, and are highly respected and spoken of with awe, especially due to high risk in creating Brightsteel. It is not uncommon for some to burn alive as they continue forging.

From [[Timeline#^2ccc71|Etherius]] onwards, most Sunsmiths are [[Lynx|Lynxes]]. Not only do they possess [[Cipher|Ciphers]] that may brave the heat, they also have ways of imbuing the Brightsteel with Etherion, enhancing it far beyond the realm of man-made weapons. Such weapons and armors are highly sought, and are extremely rare, due to both the effort it takes to make them, as well as the skill needed to wield them.
