## **Overview:**

Daemonium Theana is the First [[Demon Kings]] of the [[Helldragon]], as well the guardian/mentor of [[Apocanyser]] and [[Nerathys]], and blood mother of [[Serana]]. She is granddaughter of the [[Tyrant of Nine Dominations]],  progenitor of all dragons, and formerly of the [[Tyrant's Aiasin]], before she betrayed them for the Helldragon. She currently rules the [[White Citadel]] in the [[Umbral Chasm]].

## **Background**

Being the daughter of the dragon progenitor, she was born in his mortal image, a humanoid being of great beauty with the characteristics of the Tyrant. Her birth was momentous thing, not only due to being the daughter of the eldest son of the Tyrant, but also due to her talent as a [[Godmaiden]], a being who can birth the child of a [[Gods|God]]. 

Theana was raised for the sole purpose of becoming a mate of the Tyrant and a leader of her people. She fully supported such endeavors, believing such things were necessary to strengthen her Aiasin. However, despite all of this, she began to resent her Aiasin. She was raised to be proud of her strength, and to use it help the weak. To live for justice and find happiness in serving her people. But to Theana, that thought disgusted her. Seeing her people so eagerly trample upon their own strength, all to help those who couldn't be bothered to become strong. Yes, she wanted to explore the depths of her power, the possibilities of what she can do, and no such chances were found staying here and becoming a God's mate.

But even then, what could she do? To an [[Ais]], their [[Aiasin]], was their whole world. No other Aiasin would accept her, unless as a Godmaiden for their God. 

And that is when she came, the [[Gods#^6ece88|Incarnation]] of the Helldragon, [[Avarasin]].

As Theana was bathing in a secluded lake, contemplating, she met Avarasin. She could recognize at a glance that the strange, beautiful woman was her Aiasin's hated foe, even as limited her power was. But within the woman, Theana saw a chance, a chance to escape her fate.

And so they began talking. They would meet often, and discuss various things. And when Theana finally expressed her innermost conflict, Avarasin replied:

*Are you slave? A slave to destiny, a slave to fate? When ORDER created LAW, do you know what his first and greatest edict was? The strong shall oppress the weak.

*Strength is the ability to oppose all that stand in your way, even if it be fate itself. Your strength should be used solely how you wish it to be used. But to not use it is to be weak, to be a slave. 

*So, answer me. Are you a slave?*

And that is when Theana betrayed her Aiasin, shattering their hopes to become the Helldragon's First Demon King. 

## **Appearance**

<div style="display: block; overflow: auto;">

Theana, while having had many of her attributes stripped away with her advent to demonhood, yet retains some vestiges of her former self. Her face still holds on to the graceful features of her progeny, yet any and all softness has been stripped away from it, leaving a cold, statuesque appearance. It is reminiscent almost of the moon in its clear, yet distant beauty. Her eyes only serve to further enhance such an image - each a hard, onyx black that seldom betrays emotion. A crown-like set of horns grow back from her temples. The first pair circle around the back of her head, while the second pair grow over it. They look to be made of stone, and are chalk-white with cracks and veins of pulsing black running through - the Helldragon's corruptive nature.  Framing such features is her hair, falling like an obsidian curtain to her ankles. It flows freely, gently rippling despite the absence of wind. A single brooch sits in the back of her head, tying back the hair that would veil her face.  

Her mighty stature, reaching almost seven and a half feet, only accents her divinity, and is accompanied by a firm musculature honed from eons of adversity. Accompanying such a physique are voluptuous curves, though they do not do much to derail Theana's intimidating form - especially considering the ongoing influence of the Helldragon on her body. Where once smooth white scales covered her arms, legs and torso, veins and cracks of pure black run across them, warping the scales. Yet, by the those of the Umbral Chasm, it is seen as a blessing rather than a curse. 

###### Clothing:

Theana has little to no desire for extraneous clothing, believing there to be reason to hide her form, which in itself is a symbol of her long-standing devotion to the Helldragon. Still, some small level of decorum must be maintained, and so she chooses to wear a simple, austere dress, one that serves only for ease of movement and function.
