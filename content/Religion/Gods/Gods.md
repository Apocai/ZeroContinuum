Gods are great, conceptual beings that inhabit [[Creation]]. There are many Gods, some worshipped, some feared and some forgotten. They are called Divine Fathers by their children the [[Ais]]. 

They live throughout [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms]], most slumbering in the temples of their [[Aiasin]], and at times will try to influence the [[Lesser Realms]], either themselves or through their children (though the reason for this is unknown). -However, it is important to note that they cannot directly involve themselves with the Lesser Realms; lest they break the [[LAW]] of [[ORDER]]. 

Gods have immortal souls and cannot truly die. Bodies the Gods had before ascending are transformed, becoming the very culmination of the Lawbreaker's ideals, a body that is representative of what they have achieved and what they have broken. Most have forms far beyond human comprehension, and rarely ever reveal their true forms.

##### Incarnations:

Gods can remove a small, infinitesimal to act as their incarnate, known as an **Incarnation**. They are not strictly the Gods themselves, and function more as alter-egos comprehendible to man.  

These Incarnations often do not possess the insanity of their true forms, and can speak and talk. Still, they are beyond any other beings, and have power below only that of their true forms. 

Incarnations are formed to convey a God's will, or to go where they cannot. Incarnations are at all times aware of their true forms' intentions and thoughts, though they rarely share such things with others. A God can only have one Incarnation at a time, and their Incarnations tend to look somewhat like what they did before they ascended. 

##### Worship:

The worship of beings, while not a requirement to maintain a God's existence, greatly serves to stabilize them. With their great power, comes greater insanity, necessitating some form of confirmation to maintain their sense of self. 

Strictly speaking, worship itself is not needed. All that is needed is to have the God's presence felt in the minds of others. Being feared works equally well. 

However, when a [[Lynx]] forms a special connection with a God  through a ritual known as the [[Chain#^a42976|Shackling]], they can obtain a [[Chain]] - a bond between them and their Master that will allow them a small seed of their power, one that can grow in power alongside them.   

##### Origin:

-Gods are beings who have broken ORDER's LAW, (either now or in a previous [[Continuum Infinus|cycle]]). The act itself is impossible; therefore by breaking LAW they have surpassed possibility itself. And by doing so, the lawbreaker's soul is immortalized by ORDER and imprisoned in the [[Realms/Greater Realms/Greater Realms]], becoming something above all else at the cost of eternal torment - a God.

Gods, having immortal souls, are not reincarnated in [[Lost Paradise]]; instead their souls are consumed by the [[UNKNOWN]] so that they may ascend as the Gods of Lost Paradise. 

Even still, the Gods of Creation "live", their souls experiencing agony as they are endlessly devoured by the new Gods throughout the Lost Paradise's lifespan. And when Creation is born anew, they are forced back into the Greater Realms.

Before they ascended to Godhood, Gods were beings from all walks of life, and from all cycles. They could have been human from an ancient civilization on Zenelith, or a lifeform extinct in the current cycle. They are united only in their greatest feat and crime: breaking LAW, whether knowingly or unknowingly.

#### Power (Anti-Laws)

The Gods are beings beyond logic, and as such are night omnipotent, only able to potentially be harmed by the [[UNKNOWN]] (who are something like their antithesis) or the [[Abominations]], who are anomalies belonging to neither Creation nor Lost Paradise. 

Their immortal souls render them completely immortal, and their very being corrupts Etherion in such a way that most will be driven utterly insane, or even die, just by being near them or noticing them.

Every God possesses a special ability known as an **Anti-Law**, which is a warped versions of an Edict from LAW, the very Edict that the God broke. 

It is not known the true extent of an Anti-Law's power, but it is something that forms the very core of a God's being, and allows them control over a corrupted version of their Edict.

##### Life in Greater Realms
Gods spend much of their time in slumber, or by sending small fragments of themselves to the Lesser Realms as dreams. However, some also have their [[Aiasin]], their own clan made up those that share their blood.

##### Intelligence

The Gods are beings that have experienced an untold amount of cycles, each lasting an uncountable number of years. It is only natural that most are utterly insane or void of thought. Their lucidity and stability depends on other's awareness of them. Those who are widely thought about, either through worship or fear, have greater stability and can at times even commune normally. On the other hand, those who have been completely forgotten are stripped of intelligence and will, falling into an eternal slumber, a state known as the Long Sleep. In rare cases, where they refuse to be forgotten, they travel to the [[Auht Cealmin]], where they become beings knows as the [[Great Hermits]].

##### Breeding 

It is important to note that [[LAW]] states a new soul cannot be created. In addition, the offspring of Gods are mortal, as souls can only be made immortal by ORDER. 

Offspring can only be created between a God and a [[Godmaiden]]; Gods are unable to breed amongst each other. Godmaidens are mortal (usually Ais), who can birth a God's child safely. Such a child's soul will be drawn from the [[Gwinvarga]] like all other beings, though they will be divinely blessed with great power from their heritage. Often times, they will start their own progeny, instantly becoming one of the highest ranked Ais within their Aiasin, alongside other direct children of their God. 

#### Udra

^de9d95
All God have their names in full uppercase, as speaking their name carries an automatic enunciation with it, making it sound the same regardless of what language the speaker speaks. This is known as the **Udra**

The names of the Gods are not their actual names, but rather titles that have taken the place of their names. 

Their true names, the names carried from before their ascension, have been cursed, so that they can no longer be uttered or even thought of. Indeed, when someone utters the name of a God, what they say is the God's true name, but it is perceived by everyone including the speaker as the God's title. 

- For instance, say a God's true name was Zero, and his title is ONE. People will believe the name to be ONE, but what they are actually speak out loud is Zero, only everyone hears it as ONE. This "warping" of the name extends all the way from animals to technology. Very rarely, there are people who are born immune to the warping. However, such a thing is not a blessing but a curse, as those people tend to go mad.
